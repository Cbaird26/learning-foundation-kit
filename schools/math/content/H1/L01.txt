@article
A **signed number** carries a direction as well as a size. Positive numbers lie to the right of $0$ on the number line and negative numbers lie to the left. You already use them: a temperature of $-5^\circ\text{C}$, an overdrawn account at $-40$ dollars, a dive to $-12$ meters.

### Order and absolute value

Numbers increase as you move right, so $-7 < -2$ even though $7 > 2$: further left means smaller. The **absolute value** $|a|$ is the distance from $a$ to $0$, so it is never negative:

$$|-6| = 6, \qquad |6| = 6, \qquad |0| = 0.$$

The **opposite** of $a$ is $-a$, its mirror image across $0$. The opposite of $-4$ is $-(-4) = 4$.

### Adding

Think of a bank balance: positive numbers are deposits and negative numbers are withdrawals.

- **Same signs:** add the absolute values and keep the sign. $-3 + (-6) = -9$ (two withdrawals).
- **Different signs:** subtract the smaller absolute value from the larger, and keep the sign of the number with the larger absolute value. $-8 + 5 = -3$ (withdraw 8, deposit 5: still 3 short).

### Subtracting is adding the opposite

$$a - b = a + (-b).$$

So $4 - 9 = 4 + (-9) = -5$, and $-2 - (-7) = -2 + 7 = 5$. Taking away a debt of 7 leaves you 7 better off.

### Multiplying and dividing

| signs of the two numbers | sign of the product or quotient |
|---|---|
| the same | positive |
| different | negative |

So $(-6)(-4) = 24$, $(-6)(4) = -24$, $\frac{-18}{3} = -6$ and $\frac{-18}{-3} = 6$. With several factors, count the negative signs: an even count gives a positive result and an odd count gives a negative one. Division by zero is undefined.

Why is a negative times a negative positive? Watch the pattern $(-3)(2) = -6$, $(-3)(1) = -3$, $(-3)(0) = 0$. Each time the second factor drops by $1$, the product rises by $3$, so the next entry must be $(-3)(-1) = 3$.

### Order of operations

Work in this order: brackets; exponents; multiplication and division from left to right; addition and subtraction from left to right. Absolute value bars and fraction bars act like brackets, so work out what is inside them first.

An exponent applies only to what it touches:

$$-3^2 = -(3 \cdot 3) = -9, \qquad (-3)^2 = (-3)(-3) = 9.$$

When you want a negative number raised to a power, the brackets are essential.

@example
title: Order of operations with negatives
problem: Evaluate $-4 + 3(-2)^2 - (-12) \div 4$.
step: Powers first: $(-2)^2 = (-2)(-2) = 4$, so the expression becomes $-4 + 3(4) - (-12) \div 4$.
step: Multiply and divide from left to right: $3(4) = 12$ and $(-12) \div 4 = -3$, giving $-4 + 12 - (-3)$.
step: Rewrite subtracting $-3$ as adding $3$: $-4 + 12 + 3$.
step: Add from left to right: $-4 + 12 = 8$ and $8 + 3 = 11$.

@example
title: A change in temperature
problem: At 6 a.m. the temperature was $-7^\circ\text{C}$. By noon it was $5^\circ\text{C}$. By how much did it change?
step: A change is always the final value minus the starting value: $5 - (-7)$.
step: Subtracting $-7$ means adding its opposite: $5 + 7$.
step: $5 + 7 = 12$, so the temperature rose by $12^\circ\text{C}$.
step: Check on a number line: from $-7$ up to $0$ is $7$ degrees, and from $0$ up to $5$ is $5$ more, $12$ in all.

@mistakes
- Treating $-3^2$ as $9$. The exponent applies only to the $3$, so $-3^2 = -9$; only $(-3)^2 = 9$.
- Adding absolute values when the signs differ, as in $-8 + 5 = -13$. The correct result is $-3$.
- Changing the sign of the wrong number when rewriting subtraction: $a - b = a + (-b)$, so only the second number changes.
- Thinking "two negatives make a positive" in addition: $-4 + (-6) = -10$, not $10$. The rule is about multiplying and dividing.

@practice num
prompt: Compute $-9 + 4$.
answer: -5
check: -9 + 4
hint: The signs are different, so subtract the absolute values: $9 - 4$.
hint: The number with the larger absolute value is $-9$. Which sign does the answer take?
solution: $9 - 4 = 5$, and $-9$ has the larger absolute value, so the result is $-5$. On a number line: start at $-9$ and move $4$ to the right.

@practice num
prompt: Compute $6 - (-11)$.
answer: 17
check: 6 - (-11)
hint: Rewrite the subtraction as adding the opposite.
hint: The opposite of $-11$ is $11$.
solution: $6 - (-11) = 6 + 11 = 17$.

@practice num
prompt: Compute $\frac{(-6)(-5)}{-3}$.
answer: -10
check: (-6)*(-5)/(-3)
hint: Work out the top first: a negative times a negative is positive.
hint: Then divide a positive number by a negative number. What sign results?
solution: $(-6)(-5) = 30$ and $\frac{30}{-3} = -10$. There are three negative signs in all (an odd count), so the result is negative.

@practice mc
prompt: Which expression equals $-16$?
choice: $(-2)^4$
choice*: $-2^4$
choice: $(-4)(-4)$
choice: $-8 - (-8)$
hint: In $-2^4$ the exponent touches only the $2$.
hint: Evaluate each choice separately, and remember that subtracting a negative means adding.
solution: $-2^4 = -(2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2) = -16$. The others: $(-2)^4 = 16$ (four negative factors), $(-4)(-4) = 16$, and $-8 - (-8) = -8 + 8 = 0$.

@practice num
prompt: Evaluate $\frac{-4 - 2(-7)}{(-1)^3 - 4}$.
answer: -2
check: (-4 - 2*(-7))/((-1)**3 - 4)
hint: Treat the fraction bar like brackets: simplify the top and the bottom separately.
hint: Top: multiply before subtracting, $2(-7) = -14$. Bottom: $(-1)^3 = -1$.
solution: Top: $-4 - (-14) = -4 + 14 = 10$. Bottom: $-1 - 4 = -5$. So the value is $\frac{10}{-5} = -2$.

@quiz num
prompt: A diver is at $-18$ m, that is, 18 m below the surface. The diver rises 7 m and then descends 12 m. What is the diver's new position?
unit: m
answer: -23
check: -18 + 7 - 12
hint: Rising adds a positive number; descending subtracts one.
hint: Compute $-18 + 7$ first, then subtract $12$.
solution: $-18 + 7 = -11$, then $-11 - 12 = -23$. The diver is at $-23$ m, 23 m below the surface.

@quiz num
prompt: Evaluate $|-7 + 2| - 3^2 \cdot (-2) \div 6$.
answer: 8
check: abs(-7 + 2) - 3**2*(-2)/6
hint: Absolute value bars act like brackets: $|-7 + 2| = |-5|$.
hint: Next comes the power $3^2$; then multiply and divide from left to right: $9 \cdot (-2) \div 6$.
solution: $|-5| = 5$. Then $9 \cdot (-2) = -18$ and $-18 \div 6 = -3$. Finally $5 - (-3) = 5 + 3 = 8$.
