@article
The United States is a country in North America. It has **50 states**. Its capital city is **Washington, D.C.**, which is not part of any state. Most states touch each other, but two do not: **Alaska** is far to the northwest and **Hawaii** is a group of islands in the Pacific Ocean.

### Big landforms and waters

- The **Rocky Mountains** are tall mountains in the west.
- The **Appalachian Mountains** are older, lower mountains in the east.
- The **Mississippi River** is a long river that flows south to the Gulf of Mexico.
- The **Great Lakes** are five huge lakes in the north.
- The **Atlantic Ocean** is on the east coast and the **Pacific Ocean** is on the west coast.

### Regions

A **region** is a group of places that are close together and share some things in common. People often group the states into five regions:

1. **Northeast**: cities, forests and old towns. Winters are cold and snowy.
2. **Southeast**: warm weather, beaches and farms.
3. **Midwest**: many farms and flat plains. It is in the middle of the country.
4. **Southwest**: hot, dry land, deserts and canyons.
5. **West**: tall mountains, forests and a long Pacific coast.

Some books group the regions a little differently. That is okay, as long as you say how you are grouping them.

### Try this

Find a map of the United States. Find the state where you live and tell which region it is in. Then find the Mississippi River with your finger.

@example
title: Find the region
problem: A state has hot, dry deserts and big canyons. Which region fits best?
step: Look at the clues: hot, dry, deserts, canyons.
step: These clues match the Southwest.
step: The answer is the Southwest.

@example
title: Count the regions
problem: You have named Northeast, Southeast and Midwest. How many regions are left to name?
step: There are 5 regions.
step: You have named 3.
step: 5 - 3 = 2. Southwest and West are left.

@mistakes
- Thinking Washington, D.C. is a state. It is the capital city and not a state.
- Thinking all of the United States touches. Alaska and Hawaii are apart from the rest.
- Mixing up the two big mountain ranges: the Rockies are in the west and the Appalachians are in the east.
