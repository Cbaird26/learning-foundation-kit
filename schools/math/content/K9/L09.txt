@article
Long before ships came from Europe, many Native nations lived across North America. In 1492, a voyage from Spain reached islands in the Americas. Soon, people from Spain, France, England and the Netherlands sailed here to find land, trade goods and riches. Their coming changed life for the Native nations. Many Native people became sick with diseases from Europe, and many lost their land.

### Colonies

A **colony** is a place ruled by a faraway country. England began a colony at **Jamestown**, Virginia, in **1607**. In **1620**, a group of English families, who wanted to practice their religion in their own way, started the Plymouth colony in what is now Massachusetts. They landed where the **Wampanoag** people had lived for thousands of years. The Wampanoag helped the newcomers with food and farming for a time. Later, there was conflict over land.

### Africans in the colonies

In 1619, the first recorded Africans were brought to Virginia by force. Over many years, many thousands of people were taken from Africa and **enslaved** in the colonies. Enslaved people were treated as property and were not paid. They worked hard and also kept their languages, music and stories.

### The 13 colonies

By the 1700s, England (called Great Britain after 1707) had **13 colonies** along the Atlantic coast. They were in three groups: the New England, Middle and Southern colonies. Colonists came for many reasons: land, work, religion and trade. Some came by choice. Others, like enslaved Africans, did not.

### Different points of view

A colonist might see new chances. A Native person might see a loss of home. An enslaved person might see a loss of freedom. To understand history, we listen to many people.

@example
title: Put it in order
problem: Which came first: Jamestown (1607) or Plymouth (1620)?
step: Compare the years: 1607 and 1620.
step: 1607 is smaller, so it is earlier.
step: Jamestown came first.

@example
title: How many years apart?
problem: Jamestown started in 1607. Plymouth started in 1620. About how many years apart is that?
step: Subtract the years: 1620 - 1607.
step: 1620 - 1607 = 13.
step: They are 13 years apart.

@mistakes
- Saying the land was empty. Native nations already lived here.
- Leaving out the people who were enslaved. They were part of colonial history.
- Mixing up the order of Jamestown (1607) and Plymouth (1620).
