@article
A **percent** is a ratio out of $100$, so $8\%$ means $\frac{8}{100} = 0.08$. Percent problems are proportional relationships: the tax or tip is a fixed multiple of the price. Here the constant of proportionality is the percent written as a decimal.

### Tax and tips

Sales tax and tips are **added** to the price.

$$\text{tax} = \text{rate} \times \text{price}, \qquad \text{total} = \text{price} + \text{tax}.$$

A \$20 meal with an $18\%$ tip has a tip of $0.18 \cdot 20 = 3.60$ dollars and a total of \$23.60.

### Discounts

A discount is **subtracted** from the price. A $25\%$ discount on a \$40 item takes off $0.25 \cdot 40 = 10$ dollars, so the sale price is \$30.

### Multipliers: one step instead of two

Adding $8\%$ tax means you pay $100\% + 8\% = 108\%$ of the price, so multiply by $1.08$. Taking off $20\%$ means you pay $80\%$, so multiply by $0.80$.

$$\text{new amount} = (1 \pm \text{rate}) \times \text{original}.$$

To discount and then add tax, multiply by both multipliers. A \$50 item at $20\%$ off with $5\%$ tax costs $50 \cdot 0.80 \cdot 1.05 = 42$ dollars.

### Percent change

To compare a new value with an old one, use

$$\text{percent change} = \frac{\text{new} - \text{original}}{\text{original}} \times 100\%.$$

A positive answer is a **percent increase**; a negative answer is a **percent decrease**. The key point: always divide by the **original** amount. If a price goes from \$80 to \$100, the change is $\frac{20}{80} = 25\%$.

Percent changes are not reversible by the same percent. A price that drops $50\%$ from \$100 to \$50 must rise by $\frac{50}{50} = 100\%$ to get back, because the second change is measured from the smaller number.

@example
title: Discount, then tax
problem: A shirt costs \$30. It is on sale for $20\%$ off, and then $7\%$ sales tax is added to the sale price. What is the final cost?
step: A $20\%$ discount means you pay $80\%$ of the price: $30 \cdot 0.80 = 24$ dollars.
step: Tax of $7\%$ means you pay $107\%$ of the sale price, a multiplier of $1.07$.
step: Multiply: $24 \cdot 1.07 = 25.68$.
step: The final cost is \$25.68. As one expression: $30 \cdot 0.80 \cdot 1.07$.

@mistakes
- Dividing by the new value instead of the original value when finding percent change.
- Taking the tax on the original price after a discount, instead of on the sale price.
- Treating "$20\%$ off then $20\%$ more" as canceling out. The second percent is of a different amount.

@practice num
prompt: A \$25 item has $8\%$ sales tax. What is the total cost, in dollars?
answer: 27
check: 25 * 1.08
hint: Find the tax first, or use the multiplier $1.08$.
solution: Tax is $0.08 \cdot 25 = 2$ dollars, so the total is $25 + 2 = 27$ dollars.

@quiz num
prompt: A town grows from $1250$ people to $1500$ people. What is the percent increase?
answer: 20
unit: %
check: (1500 - 1250) / 1250 * 100
hint: Percent change is the change divided by the original amount.
solution: $\frac{1500 - 1250}{1250} = \frac{250}{1250} = 0.20$, a $20\%$ increase.
