@review
### Week by week

- **Week 1:** A summary tells the beginning, middle and end in your own words. A theme is the lesson.
- **Week 2:** An inference is clues plus what you know. Prove it with two quotations.
- **Week 3:** First person uses "I." Third person uses names and "he" or "she." Compare accounts by what is the same and different.
- **Week 4:** A simile uses like or as. A metaphor says one thing is another.
- **Week 5:** Five structures: description, sequence, compare and contrast, cause and effect, problem and solution.
- **Week 6:** Prefixes, roots and suffixes help you work out new words.
- **Week 7:** Nouns, verbs, adjectives and adverbs. The subject and verb agree.
- **Week 8:** Join sentences with a comma and and, but, so or or. Use apostrophes carefully.
- **Week 9:** Five paragraphs with a main idea.
- **Week 10:** Opinion, reasons, and facts.
- **Week 11:** Ask a question, use two sources and take short notes.
- **Week 12:** Cite sources and present clearly.

### Self-check

- Can I tell a topic from a theme?
- Can I show an inference with two quotations?
- Can I explain a simile in plain words?
- Do my verbs agree with my subjects?

### Common errors

Writing a topic instead of a theme, copying notes word for word, forgetting the comma before a joining word, and forgetting to name a source.

@exam mc
prompt: Read: "Pat's plant was droopy. Pat had forgotten to water it all week. Then Pat poured water on it, and the leaves stood up again." Which is the best theme?
choice: Plants are green.
choice*: Taking care of things helps them thrive.
choice: Pat has a plant.
choice: Water is clear.
hint: Think about what Pat learned.
solution: Watering made the plant better, so the lesson is that caring for things helps them. The other choices are only details or topics.

@exam mc
prompt: Which sentence has a simile, correct commas and a joining word?
choice: The wind was a lion it roared.
choice*: The wind roared like a lion, and the trees shook.
choice: The wind roared like a lion and, the trees shook.
choice: The wind roared like a lion the trees shook.
hint: Look for like, a comma and a joining word.
solution: It has a simile ("like a lion"), a comma and the joining word "and."

@exam num
prompt: You write a report with 1 introduction, 3 body paragraphs and 1 conclusion. Each body paragraph has 4 sentences. The introduction and conclusion have 3 sentences each. How many sentences are in your report?
answer: 18
check: 3 * 4 + 3 + 3
hint: Find the body sentences first, then add the other two paragraphs.
solution: Body: 3 times 4 is 12. Add 3 and 3 to get 18.
