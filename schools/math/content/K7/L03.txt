@lesson
title: What plants need
summary: Name what plants need to grow and name the main parts of a plant.
objective: Say that plants need water, light, air and space to grow.
objective: Name the roots, stem, leaves and flower of a plant.
objective: Grow a bean seed and record how it changes.
reading: NASA Space Place: look up "What is photosynthesis?" or ask your grown-up to find a picture book about how plants grow.

@article
Plants are living things. To grow, a plant needs four things:

- **Water.** Plants drink water through their roots.
- **Light.** Plants use sunlight to make their own food.
- **Air.** Leaves take in air.
- **Space.** A plant needs room for its roots and leaves.

### Parts of a plant

- **Roots** hold the plant in the soil and drink water.
- The **stem** holds the plant up and carries water to the leaves.
- **Leaves** catch sunlight.
- A **flower** can make seeds. A seed can grow into a new plant.

### Grow a bean

With your grown-up, wet a paper towel and put a dry bean in it. Put the towel in a clear bag in a sunny window. Keep the towel damp. After a few days a root grows out. Later a stem and leaves grow. Draw the bean each day.

@example
title: A plant in a dark closet
problem: Your grown-up puts a plant in a dark closet for a week. What happens, and why?
step: The plant gets no light. Plants need light to make food.
step: After a week the leaves turn pale and droop. The plant is not getting what it needs.
step: When the plant goes back by a window, it can get better.

@mistakes
- Thinking plants eat food from the soil the way animals eat. Plants make their own food using light, water and air.
- Thinking the roots are at the top. Roots grow down into the soil.
