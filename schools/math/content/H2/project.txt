step: Place a right-triangle flower bed in one corner with legs of $6$ m along the short side and $8$ m along the long side. Use the Pythagorean theorem to find its hypotenuse, and use $\tan\theta$ to find its two acute angles to the nearest tenth of a degree.

@review
## Week-by-week key ideas

1. **Reasoning.** Points, lines, rays, segments; angle pairs (complementary $90^\circ$, supplementary $180^\circ$, linear pair, vertical); converse, inverse, contrapositive; one counterexample disproves a claim.
2. **Parallel lines.** Corresponding, alternate interior and alternate exterior angles are equal; same-side interior angles are supplementary; converses prove lines parallel. Proofs need a reason for every line.
3. **Congruence.** SSS, SAS, ASA, AAS, HL; not SSA, not AAA. Match vertices in order; CPCTC afterward.
4. **Triangle proofs.** Angle sum $180^\circ$; exterior angle equals the sum of remote interior angles; isosceles base angles; perpendicular bisector theorem.
5. **Similarity.** AA, SAS and SSS similarity; proportional corresponding sides; parallel segment divides sides proportionally; areas scale by $k^2$.
6. **Right triangles.** $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$; 45-45-90 and 30-60-90; SOH-CAH-TOA; angles of elevation.
7. **Polygons.** Interior sum $(n-2)\cdot 180^\circ$, exterior sum $360^\circ$; parallelogram properties and tests; the quadrilateral hierarchy.
8. **Circles.** Central angle equals arc; inscribed angle is half the arc; tangent is perpendicular to the radius; intersecting chords $ab = cd$.
9. **Area.** Rectangle, parallelogram, triangle, trapezoid, circle; composite figures by adding or subtracting.
10. **Solids.** $V = Bh$ for prisms and cylinders, $\tfrac{1}{3}Bh$ for pyramids and cones, sphere $\tfrac{4}{3}\pi r^3$; volume scales by $k^3$.
11. **Coordinates.** Distance, midpoint, slope; parallel means equal slopes, perpendicular means product $-1$; circle equation.
12. **Transformations.** Translations, reflections and rotations preserve size; dilations preserve shape; symmetry.

## Self-check list

- I can give a reason for every line of a proof.
- I can tell which angle pairs are equal and which are supplementary.
- I can choose between SSS, SAS, ASA and AAS and explain why SSA fails.
- I can set up a similarity proportion with corresponding sides.
- I can choose sine, cosine or tangent and solve for a side or an angle.
- I can compute area and volume with correct units.

## Most common errors

- Using SSA or AAA as congruence criteria.
- Matching non-corresponding sides in a proportion.
- Multiplying areas by $k$ instead of $k^2$, or volumes by $k^2$ instead of $k^3$.
- Using the slanted side instead of the perpendicular height.
- Calculator in radian mode for degree problems.
- Inscribed angle set equal to the arc instead of half of it.

prompt: A right triangular flower bed has legs $9$ m and $12$ m. A second, similar bed has a shortest side of $6$ m. Find the area of the second bed in $\text{m}^2$.
answer: 24
check: 0.5 * (9 * 6 / 9) * (12 * 6 / 9)
hint: Find the scale factor from the shortest sides. The shortest side of the first bed is one of the legs.
hint: The scale factor is $6/9 = 2/3$, so the legs of the second bed are $6$ and $8$.
solution: $k = 6/9 = \tfrac{2}{3}$, so the legs are $6$ and $8$ and the area is $\tfrac{1}{2}(6)(8) = 24\ \text{m}^2$. Check with the area rule: $\tfrac{1}{2}(9)(12) = 54$ and $54 \times \left(\tfrac{2}{3}\right)^2 = 24$.

prompt: A model of a cylindrical container has radius $2$ cm and height $6$ cm. The real container is similar to the model with a scale factor of $5$. Find the volume of the real container in $\text{cm}^3$, in terms of $\pi$ (enter the coefficient with $\pi$, for example $5\pi$).
answer: 3000pi
check: math.pi * (5 * 2)**2 * (5 * 6)
hint: Find the real radius and height first, then use $V = \pi r^2 h$.
hint: The real radius is $10$ cm and the real height is $30$ cm.
solution: Real radius $2 \times 5 = 10$ and height $6 \times 5 = 30$, so $V = \pi (10)^2 (30) = 3000\pi\ \text{cm}^3$. Check: the model has $V = 24\pi$, and volume scales by $5^3 = 125$, so $24\pi \times 125 = 3000\pi$.

prompt: Triangle $ABC$ has vertices $A(1, 2)$, $B(5, 2)$ and $C(3, 6)$. Point $C$ is reflected across the $x$-axis, and the image is then translated by the vector $\langle 4, 1\rangle$. Find the final coordinates of the image of $C$.
answer: (7, -5)
check: (3 + 4, -6 + 1)
hint: Reflect first: the $y$-coordinate changes sign.
hint: After the reflection the point is $(3, -6)$; now add the vector.
solution: Reflection across the $x$-axis: $(3, 6) \to (3, -6)$. Translation by $\langle 4, 1\rangle$: $(3 + 4, -6 + 1) = (7, -5)$.
