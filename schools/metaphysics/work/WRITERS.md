Level notes:
- elementary: This course is for a young child with an adult helper who reads aloud; follow the brief's elementary guidance exactly: gentle topics, auto-graded items with one clearly right answer, open questions as self items whose rubrics accept any answer with a reason, and never a question about private family matters, beliefs or religion.
- middle/high, T: Every argument, truth table and number must be exactly right (check each with python3 -c); present every view fairly; never make a contested view the right answer.
- middle/high, W: Retell every text and view accurately and fairly; attribute only what you are sure of; never make a contested view the right answer of an auto-graded item; self items need complete model answers and specific rubrics.
- college, W: University level. Every attribution, claim about a text, argument in standard form and distinction must be correct; paraphrase rather than quote unless the text is public domain and you are sure of the exact wording; never make a contested view the right answer; follow the brief's list of current facts.
- college, T: University level. Every definition, proof (line by line, with the brief's rule names), truth table, model and number must be correct: check each with python3 -c; follow the brief's list of current facts.
- professional: Graduate level: an accurate, fair picture of each current debate; attribute only views and arguments you are sure of; self items need model answers a faculty reader would admire and specific rubrics; follow the brief's list of current facts.
