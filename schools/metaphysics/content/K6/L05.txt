@article
Informational texts give facts. Writers put their facts in a plan, and that plan is called a **text structure**. When you know the plan, the text is easier to understand.

### Five common structures

1. **Description:** tells what something is like. Signal words: *for example, such as, looks like.*
2. **Sequence:** tells steps or events in order. Signal words: *first, next, then, finally.*
3. **Compare and contrast:** tells how things are alike and different. Signal words: *both, same, but, different, however.*
4. **Cause and effect:** tells what happened and why. Signal words: *because, so, as a result.*
5. **Problem and solution:** tells a problem and how to fix it. Signal words: *problem, solve, answer, fix.*

### Signal words

**Signal words** are clues. They tell you which plan the writer used.

"First, wash your hands. Next, get the bread. Then add jam." The words *first, next, then* tell you it is **sequence**.

"Because it rained, the grass grew." The word *because* tells you it is **cause and effect**. The cause is the rain. The effect is that the grass grew.

### Text features

Nonfiction has special parts that help you find facts:

- **Title:** tells the main topic.
- **Heading:** a small title for one part.
- **Bold words:** important words, often in the glossary.
- **Caption:** words under a picture that explain it.
- **Table of contents:** lists the parts and their pages.
- **Glossary:** tells what hard words mean.

If you want a fact about how tadpoles grow, look at the headings first. Then read only that part.

@example
title: Find the text structure
problem: Read: "Cats and dogs are both pets. Cats nap a lot, but dogs need walks." What is the text structure?
step: Look for signal words. "Both" and "but" show up.
step: These words show how things are alike (both pets) and different (naps versus walks).
step: The structure is compare and contrast.

@mistakes
- Guessing the structure without looking at the signal words.
- Mixing up cause and effect. The cause is why it happened, and the effect is what happened.
- Skipping headings and captions, which tell you where the facts are.
