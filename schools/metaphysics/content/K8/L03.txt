@article
An **ecosystem** is a place where living things and non-living things (like water, soil, air and sunlight) work together. A pond, a forest and a backyard are all ecosystems.

### Producers
**Producers** make their own food. Green plants use sunlight, water and air to make food. Grass, trees and pond algae are producers.

### Consumers
**Consumers** cannot make food. They eat other living things.
- **Herbivores** eat only plants. A rabbit is a herbivore.
- **Carnivores** eat only animals. A lion is a carnivore.
- **Omnivores** eat both plants and animals. A bear is an omnivore.

### Decomposers
**Decomposers** break down dead plants and animals into tiny bits that go back into the soil. Mushrooms, worms and bacteria are decomposers. The soil then helps plants grow.

### Food chains
A **food chain** shows who eats what. The arrow points to the eater and shows the food energy moving.

grass → rabbit → fox

Grass is the producer. The rabbit is a consumer that eats the grass. The fox is a consumer that eats the rabbit. All the energy begins with the Sun.

### Food webs
Most animals eat more than one thing. Many food chains join together to make a **food web**. A fox may eat rabbits and mice. A mouse may eat grass and seeds. If rabbits disappear, the fox can eat more mice, but the grass may grow too tall.

@example
title: Read a food chain
problem: Look at this chain: algae → tadpole → fish → heron. Which is the producer? Who eats the fish?
step: The producer is the one at the start that makes its own food: the algae.
step: The arrow points to the eater. The arrow goes from fish to heron, so the heron eats the fish.

@example
title: Count the links
problem: A food web has 3 plants, 4 plant eaters and 2 meat eaters. How many living things is that?
step: Write the numbers: $3$, $4$ and $2$.
step: Add them: $3 + 4 + 2 = 9$.

@mistakes
- Drawing the arrow the wrong way. The arrow points to the eater, not to the food.
- Thinking the food chain starts with an animal. It starts with a producer, and the energy comes from the Sun.
- Forgetting decomposers. They are part of every ecosystem.
