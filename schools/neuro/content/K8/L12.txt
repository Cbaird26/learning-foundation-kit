@article
Your body is made of **systems**. Each system has parts that work together to do a job. Systems also work with each other.

### Body systems
- **Skeletal system**: bones hold up your body and protect the brain, heart and lungs. An adult has 206 bones.
- **Muscular system**: muscles pull on bones to move you. Muscles come in pairs. The heart is a muscle too.
- **Respiratory system**: the nose, windpipe and **lungs**. You breathe in air with oxygen and breathe out carbon dioxide.
- **Circulatory system**: the **heart** pumps blood through tubes called blood vessels. Blood carries oxygen and food energy to every part of the body.
- **Nervous system**: the **brain**, spinal cord and nerves. Nerves carry messages. The brain controls your body and lets you think.
- **Digestive system**: breaks food into tiny pieces your body can use.

### A bite of food
Follow a bite of a cracker:
1. **Mouth**: teeth chew it and saliva makes it soft.
2. **Esophagus**: a tube that squeezes the food down to the stomach.
3. **Stomach**: a muscle bag that mixes the food with juices into a thick liquid.
4. **Small intestine**: a long, coiled tube. It breaks food down more and moves the **nutrients** through its walls into the blood.
5. **Large intestine**: it takes back water. What is left is waste, and leaves the body.

### Working together
When you run, the nervous system sends messages, muscles move bones, your lungs breathe faster, and your heart pumps faster to bring oxygen to the muscles. Digestion earlier gave the food energy.

### Staying healthy
Healthy habits help all your systems: eat a variety of foods, drink water, sleep, play and wash your hands.

@example

step: Write: $1$ beat each second for $30$ seconds.
step: Multiply: $1 \times 30 = 30$ beats.

@mistakes
- Thinking that the stomach is where all food is taken into the body. Most nutrients pass into the blood in the small intestine.
- Thinking the lungs make oxygen. They take oxygen from the air.
- Thinking systems work alone. They all work together.
