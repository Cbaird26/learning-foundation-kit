@article
A **prism** is a solid with two identical, parallel faces called **bases**, joined by rectangular faces. A box is a **rectangular prism**; a tent shape with triangular ends is a **triangular prism**. The name comes from the shape of the base.

### Volume

**Volume** measures how much space a solid takes up, in **cubic units**. For any prism,

$$V = (\text{area of the base}) \times (\text{height of the prism}).$$

Here the "height" is the distance between the two bases. For a rectangular prism with length $\ell$, width $w$ and height $h$, the base area is $\ell w$, so $V = \ell w h$. A box $5 \times 4 \times 3$ holds $60$ cubic units.

For a triangular prism, the base area is $\frac{1}{2}bh$ using the triangle's base and height, and then you multiply by the prism's length. A triangle with base $6$ and height $4$ has area $12$, and a prism of length $10$ has volume $120$.

### Surface area

**Surface area** is the total area of all the faces, in **square units**. Imagine unfolding the solid flat: this flat shape is called a **net**. Find the area of each piece and add them.

For a rectangular prism,

$$SA = 2(\ell w + \ell h + wh).$$

A cube with side $3$ has six faces of area $9$, so $SA = 54$.

For a triangular prism, add the areas of the two triangles to the areas of the three rectangles. The rectangles' widths are the triangle's three sides, and their length is the prism's length.

### Volume and capacity

Liquids are measured in liters. Since $1000\text{ cm}^3 = 1\text{ L}$, divide cubic centimeters by $1000$. A tank of $20\,000\text{ cm}^3$ holds $20$ L. Also, $1\text{ m}^3 = 1000\text{ L}$.

### Choosing the right measure

Ask whether the question is about **filling** (volume, cubic units) or **covering** (surface area, square units). Paint, wrapping paper and sheet metal use surface area; water, sand and concrete use volume.

### Keep units consistent

Convert all lengths to the same unit before multiplying. A box measuring $2$ m by $50$ cm by $40$ cm should be written as $200$ cm by $50$ cm by $40$ cm, or $2$ m by $0.5$ m by $0.4$ m.

@example
title: Volume and surface area of a box
problem: A box measures $8$ cm by $5$ cm by $3$ cm. Find its volume and its surface area.
step: Volume: $V = 8 \cdot 5 \cdot 3 = 120$ cubic centimeters.
step: The three pairs of faces have areas $8 \cdot 5 = 40$, $8 \cdot 3 = 24$ and $5 \cdot 3 = 15$.
step: Surface area: $2(40 + 24 + 15) = 2 \cdot 79 = 158$ square centimeters.
step: Units differ: volume is in cm$^3$ (cubes), surface area in cm$^2$ (squares).

@mistakes
- Giving surface area in cubic units or volume in square units.
- Forgetting the factor $2$ in the surface area formula, or leaving out a face.
- Using the slanted side of a triangle as the triangle's height when finding its area.

@practice num
prompt: A rectangular prism measures $5$ cm by $4$ cm by $3$ cm. What is its volume in cubic centimeters?
answer: 60
unit: cubic cm
check: 5 * 4 * 3
hint: Multiply length, width and height.
solution: $V = 5 \cdot 4 \cdot 3 = 60$ cubic centimeters.

@practice num
prompt: A triangular prism has a triangular base with base $6$ cm and height $4$ cm. The prism is $10$ cm long. What is its volume in cubic centimeters?
answer: 120
unit: cubic cm
check: 0.5 * 6 * 4 * 10
hint: Find the area of the triangle first.
hint: The triangle's area is $\frac{1}{2} \cdot 6 \cdot 4 = 12$.
solution: Base area is $12$ cm$^2$, so $V = 12 \cdot 10 = 120$ cubic centimeters.

@practice num
prompt: What is the surface area of a cube with side length $3$ cm, in square centimeters?
answer: 54
unit: square cm
check: 6 * 3**2
hint: A cube has $6$ identical square faces.
solution: Each face has area $9$, so $SA = 6 \cdot 9 = 54$ square centimeters.

@practice num
prompt: An aquarium is $50$ cm long, $30$ cm wide and $40$ cm tall. How many liters of water does it hold when full? ($1000\text{ cm}^3 = 1\text{ L}$.)
answer: 60
unit: L
check: 50 * 30 * 40 / 1000
hint: Find the volume in cubic centimeters first.
hint: The volume is $60\,000$ cubic centimeters. Now convert to liters.
solution: $V = 50 \cdot 30 \cdot 40 = 60\,000$ cm$^3$, which is $\frac{60\,000}{1000} = 60$ liters.

@practice num
prompt: What is the surface area of a rectangular prism that measures $6$ cm by $3$ cm by $2$ cm, in square centimeters?
answer: 72
unit: square cm
check: 2 * (6*3 + 6*2 + 3*2)
hint: Find the areas of the three different faces and double their sum.
hint: The face areas are $18$, $12$ and $6$.
solution: $2(18 + 12 + 6) = 2 \cdot 36 = 72$ square centimeters.

@quiz num
prompt: A tent is a triangular prism. Its triangular end has base $8$ ft and height $5$ ft, and the tent is $12$ ft long. What is the volume of air inside, in cubic feet?
answer: 240
unit: cubic ft
check: 0.5 * 8 * 5 * 12
hint: Volume is base area times length, and the base is a triangle.
solution: Triangle area is $\frac{1}{2} \cdot 8 \cdot 5 = 20$ ft$^2$, so $V = 20 \cdot 12 = 240$ cubic feet.

@quiz num
prompt: A rectangular tank is $2.5$ m long, $1.2$ m wide and $0.8$ m tall. What is its volume in cubic meters?
answer: 2.4
unit: cubic m
tol: 0.01
check: 2.5 * 1.2 * 0.8
hint: Multiply the three dimensions.
solution: $2.5 \cdot 1.2 = 3$ and $3 \cdot 0.8 = 2.4$ cubic meters.
