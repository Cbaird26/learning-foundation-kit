@lesson
title: Tens and ones
summary: Show a number as tens and ones, and read a two-digit number as bundles of ten and loose ones.
objective: Make bundles of ten and count the loose ones.
objective: Tell how many tens and how many ones are in a number up to 99.
objective: Build a number to 100 with bundles of ten.
reading: Khan Academy, Early math review: the topic "Tens and ones" (place value).

@article
Big numbers are easier to read when we put things in bundles of ten.

### Make a ten

Take 10 sticks and put a rubber band around them. That is one **ten**. Sticks that are not in a bundle are called **ones**.

### Read a number

Say you have 2 bundles of ten and 3 loose sticks. That is 20 and 3 more, or **23**.

The number 23 has two digits. The first digit, 2, tells how many **tens**. The second digit, 3, tells how many **ones**.

$$23 = 2\text{ tens and }3\text{ ones}$$

### Another way

The number 40 is 4 tens and 0 ones. The number 45 is 4 tens and 5 ones. The number 7 is 0 tens and 7 ones.

### Build a number

To build 36, make 3 bundles of ten and then add 6 loose ones. Count: 10, 20, 30, then 31, 32, 33, 34, 35, 36.

### Ten ones is one ten

When you have 10 loose ones, put a band around them. Now they are 1 ten. Ten ones make one ten.

### Try it

With your grown-up, use beans, buttons or pasta and a bowl. Do not put them in your mouth. Make some bundles of ten in small piles. Then count the piles and the leftovers.

@example
title: How many tens and ones?
problem: A number is 53. How many tens and how many ones?
step: Look at the first digit: 5. That is 5 tens.
step: Look at the second digit: 3. That is 3 ones.
step: So 53 is 5 tens and 3 ones, or 50 and 3 more.

@mistakes
- Mixing up the tens digit and the ones digit.
- Counting a bundle of ten as just one thing when counting all the sticks.
- Forgetting that a number like 40 has zero ones.
