@lesson
title: Reading sentences
summary: Read a sentence from left to right and tell what makes a sentence.
objective: Read words in a sentence from left to right.
objective: Know that a sentence starts with a capital letter and ends with . or ? or !
objective: Count the words in a sentence.
reading: Reading Rockets, "Phonics and decoding": look up the topics "fluency" and "reading sentences".

@article
A **sentence** is a group of words that tells a whole idea. "The cat sat." is a sentence. "The cat" is not, because it does not say what happened.

### Rules of a sentence

- A sentence **starts with a capital letter**.
- Words have **spaces** between them.
- A sentence **ends with a mark**: a period (.), a question mark (?), or an exclamation mark (!).

### The three end marks

- A **period** ends a telling sentence: "The dog ran."
- A **question mark** ends an asking sentence: "Can the dog run?"
- An **exclamation mark** ends a strong feeling: "The dog ran fast!"

### How to read a sentence

1. Start at the left. Move to the right, one word at a time.
2. Slide your finger under the words.
3. Sound out words you do not know. Use sight words you do know.
4. At the end mark, stop. For a question, let your voice go up.
5. Read it again so it sounds smooth, like talking.

### Try it with your grown-up

Read this together: "The red hen sat on a mat." Put your finger under each word. Count the words. Then read it again, faster and smoother.

@example
title: Read and count
problem: Read "The red hen sat on a mat." How many words does it have?
step: Slide your finger under each word as you read: The, red, hen, sat, on, a, mat.
step: Count each word as your finger moves: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7.
step: The sentence has 7 words and ends with a period.

@mistakes
- Skipping words. Slide your finger under every word.
- Forgetting to stop at the end mark.
- Reading each word slowly, like a list. Read it again to make it smooth.

@practice mc
prompt: Which one is a sentence?
choice: The big dog
choice*: The big dog ran.
choice: ran fast
choice: on the bed
hint: A sentence tells a whole idea and ends with a mark.
solution: "The big dog ran." tells a whole idea: who, and what happened.

@practice mc
prompt: What mark goes at the end? "Can you see the sun"
choice: A period (.)
choice*: A question mark (?)
choice: A comma (,)
choice: No mark
hint: Is this sentence telling or asking?
solution: It asks something, so it ends with a question mark.

@practice num
prompt: How many words are in this sentence: "I can see a big red bus."?
answer: 7
check: len("I can see a big red bus.".split())
hint: Point to each word and count it.
solution: I, can, see, a, big, red, bus is 7 words.

@practice num
prompt: How many sentences are in this: "The sun is up. We get up. We run to the park."?
answer: 3
check: len("The sun is up. We get up. We run to the park.".split(". "))
hint: Each sentence ends with a period.
hint: Count the periods.
solution: There are 3 periods, so 3 sentences.

rubric: The second reading was smoother than the first.
