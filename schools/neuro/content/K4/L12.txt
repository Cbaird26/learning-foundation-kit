@lesson
title: Writing words and sentences
summary: Write words by listening for sounds, and write a sentence with a capital, spaces and an end mark.
objective: Write a word by saying it slowly and writing a letter for each sound.
objective: Write a sentence that starts with a capital letter and ends with a period.
objective: Write two sentences about a story you read.
reading: Reading Rockets, "Phonics and decoding": look up the topics "spelling" and "writing sentences".

@article
Reading and writing go together. When you write, you use the same sounds, backwards. You listen to a word and write the letters.

### Write a word

1. Say the word slowly.
2. Hold up a finger for each sound.
3. Write a letter (or team) for each sound.
4. Read it back.

For "ship": /sh/ /i/ /p/. Write sh, i, p. Ship.

### Write a sentence

A sentence needs three things.

- A **capital letter** at the start.
- **Spaces** between words. Leave a finger-width gap.
- An **end mark**, like a period.

Example: "The dog ran." Start with T. Space after each word. End with a period.

### Write about a story

After you read a story, write two sentences about it. Say your sentence out loud first. Count the words on your fingers. Then write it.

For Pip the duck: "Pip got the ball. Pip gave it to Sam."

### Tricky words

Use your sight word cards for words like "the", "was" and "said". Copy them from the card. Do not worry if a hard word is not perfect. Keep going.

### Try it with your grown-up

Write one sentence about your day. Check three things together: capital, spaces, end mark.

@example
title: Write a sentence
problem: Write the sentence: the cat sat on the mat
step: Say it out loud and count the words: 6 words.
step: Start with a capital letter: The. Leave a space after each word.
step: End with a period: "The cat sat on the mat."

@mistakes
- Forgetting the capital letter at the start.
- Writing words with no spaces between them.
- Forgetting the end mark. Check by reading it back.
- Missing a sound in a word. Say the word slowly and check each sound.

@practice num
prompt: How many words are in the sentence "I can see the big red bus."?
answer: 7
check: len("I can see the big red bus.".split())
hint: Count each word, not each letter.
solution: I, can, see, the, big, red, bus is 7 words.
