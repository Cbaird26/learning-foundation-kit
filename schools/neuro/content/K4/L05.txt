@lesson
title: Short vowels in three-letter words
summary: Read and spell three-letter words with a short vowel in the middle.
objective: Say the short sound for a, e, i, o and u.
objective: Read words like cat, bed, pig, dog and sun.
objective: Spell a three-letter word by listening to its sounds.
reading: Reading Rockets, "Phonics and decoding": look up the topic "short vowels" and "CVC words".

@article
Many first words have three sounds: a consonant, a vowel, then a consonant. Examples are cat, bed, pig, dog and sun. The vowel is in the middle. It says its **short sound**.

### Note for the grown-up

The short vowel sounds are:

- a as in cat (/a/, like the start of "apple")
- e as in bed (/e/, like the start of "egg")
- i as in pig (/i/, like the start of "itch")
- o as in dog (/o/, like the start of "octopus")
- u as in sun (/u/, like the start of "umbrella")

### Read it

Say each sound, then blend: b-e-d, bed. Change only the middle letter and you get a new word: bat, bet, bit, bot, but. Some of these are real words and some are not. Real words make sense.

### Word families

Words that end the same make a **word family**. The -at family has cat, hat, mat, sat and bat. The -un family has sun, bun, fun and run.

### Spell it

To spell a word, say it slowly. Write a letter for each sound. For "pig", write p, then i, then g.

### Try it with your grown-up

Set out three small things from home, such as a cup, a sock and a pen. Say each name slowly and write its sounds. Then check the middle vowel.

@example
title: Spell by sounds
problem: Spell the word "hen".
step: Say it slowly: /h/ /e/ /n/. There are 3 sounds.
step: Write a letter for each sound: h, e, n.
step: Read it back to check: h-e-n, hen.

@mistakes
- Guessing the middle vowel. Slow down and listen to the vowel sound.
- Reading the vowel by its name. In "bed", e does not say "ee".
- Skipping the last letter. Check you wrote a letter for every sound.

@practice num
prompt: Here are five words: cat, dog, sun, pig, hen. How many have the short u sound in the middle?
answer: 1
check: sum("u" in w for w in ["cat", "dog", "sun", "pig", "hen"])
hint: Short u sounds like the start of "umbrella".
solution: Only "sun" has the short u. That makes 1.

@practice num
prompt: Here are six words: cap, map, pen, hat, bag, sit. How many have a short a in the middle?
answer: 4
check: sum(w[1] == "a" for w in ["cap", "map", "pen", "hat", "bag", "sit"])
hint: Say each word. Short a sounds like the start of "apple".
hint: Cap, map, hat and bag all have an a. Check pen and sit.
solution: Cap, map, hat and bag have a short a. Pen has e and sit has i. That makes 4.

@practice self
prompt: Say these sounds slowly, then write the word: /p/ /o/ /t/. Then write two more words that rhyme with it.
hint: Write one letter for each sound.
solution: The word is pot. Words that rhyme with pot: hot, dot, cot, not.
rubric: You wrote p, o, t in order for the first word.
rubric: Your two new words both end in "-ot".
rubric: You read each word aloud to your grown-up.
