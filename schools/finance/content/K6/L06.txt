@article
Big words are often small parts stuck together. If you know the parts, you can work out the word.

### Three kinds of word parts

- A **root** is the main part with the main meaning.
- A **prefix** goes at the start of a word and changes its meaning.
- A **suffix** goes at the end of a word and can change its meaning or its job.

### Common prefixes

| Prefix | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| un- | not | unhappy (not happy) |
| re- | again | redo (do again) |
| pre- | before | preview (see before) |
| dis- | not, opposite | dislike (not like) |
| bi- | two | bicycle (two wheels) |

### Common suffixes

| Suffix | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| -ful | full of | joyful (full of joy) |
| -less | without | careless (without care) |
| -er | one who | teacher (one who teaches) |
| -able | can be | washable (can be washed) |

### Greek and Latin roots

Many English roots come from old Greek and Latin words.

| Root | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| tele (Greek) | far | telephone (sound from far) |
| photo (Greek) | light | photograph (light picture) |
| aud (Latin) | hear | audio (to do with hearing) |
| port (Latin) | carry | transport (carry across) |
| scrib (Latin) | write | describe (write about) |

### Take a word apart

Look at **unreadable**. The parts are *un-* (not), *read* (root) and *-able* (can be). Put the meanings together: "not able to be read."

If a word is new, cover the ends, find the root, and then add each part.

@example
title: Work out a new word
problem: What does "rewrite" mean? What does "telescope" suggest if tele means far and scope means look?
step: Break "rewrite" into re- (again) and write. It means "write again."
step: Break "telescope" into tele (far) and scope (look).
step: Put the meanings together: a telescope is something you use to look at faraway things.

@mistakes
- Thinking every word that starts with "re" has the prefix re-. "Reach" does not mean "to each again."
- Forgetting the suffix. Careful means "full of care," but careless means "without care."
- Skipping the root. The root carries the main meaning.
