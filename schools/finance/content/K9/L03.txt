@article
A **map** is a drawing of a place as seen from above. A map can show a room, a school, a town or the whole world.

### Parts of a map

- The **title** tells what the map shows.
- The **map key** (or legend) shows what each picture or color means. A little tree might mean a park. A blue color might mean water.
- The **compass rose** shows directions: **north (N)**, **south (S)**, **east (E)** and **west (W)**.

A trick to remember the order going around is "**N**ever **E**at **S**oggy **W**affles."

### Directions

North is usually at the top of a map. If you face north, east is on your right, and west is on your left. South is behind you.

### Globes

A **globe** is a model of the Earth. It is round, like the Earth. A flat map can show a lot of detail and is easy to carry, but flattening the round Earth changes some shapes and sizes a little. A globe shows the true shape better.

The **equator** is an imaginary line around the middle of the Earth. It splits the globe into two halves: north and south. The **North Pole** is at the top of the Earth, and the **South Pole** is at the bottom.

### Try this

With a grown-up, make a map of your room. Draw the door, the bed and the window as simple shapes. Add a key and a compass rose with N at the side where the window is, if the window faces north. If you do not know, ask your grown-up to help you find north.

@example
title: Using a map key
problem: On a town map, the key shows a green square for "park" and a red triangle for "fire station." You see 2 green squares and 1 red triangle. What do they show?
step: Find the picture in the key.
step: Green squares mean parks. There are 2 of them.
step: The red triangle means a fire station. There is 1.

@example
title: Using directions
problem: On a map, the school is at the top and the library is at the bottom. North is at the top. Which direction do you go from the school to the library?
step: North is at the top, so south is at the bottom.
step: The library is at the bottom.
step: You go south.

@mistakes
- Thinking north is always up in real life. North is a direction on the Earth; maps just usually put it at the top.
- Forgetting to read the key. The same picture can mean different things on different maps.
- Thinking a globe and a flat map are the same. A globe is round and a map is flat.
