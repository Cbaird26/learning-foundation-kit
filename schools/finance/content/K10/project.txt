@project
title: My saving plan
overview: You will choose something to save for, find its price, and make a weekly plan with earning, saving and spending columns. You keep the chart for four weeks, with real coins or pretend money, and then tell your grown-up how close you came and what you would change.
goal: Choose a savings goal and find out how much it costs.
goal: Make a weekly plan with earn, save and spend columns.
goal: Keep a chart for four weeks and add up the totals correctly.
goal: Explain how close you came and what you would change.
step: Choose something to save for, such as a book or a toy. Ask your grown-up to help you find a price. The price should be under \$20. Write the name and the price at the top of a big sheet of paper.
step: Decide how much money you will earn each week. It can be pretend pay for jobs, such as 50¢ for each chore. Write the amount for each week.
step: Make your plan for each week. Split the money into Save and Spend (and Give if you like). Make sure the parts add up to what you earned.
step: Draw a table with columns Week, Earn, Save, Spend and Total saved. Fill in the plan for weeks 1 to 4.
step: For four weeks, keep the chart. Each week write what really happened. Use real coins if your grown-up agrees, or use pretend money.
step: After week 4, add up your Total saved. Take it away from the price. How close did you come to your goal?
step: Show your chart to your grown-up. Tell them what went well, what was hard, and one thing you would change next time.
material: Here is a chart you can copy onto paper. The first row is filled in as an example for a pretend plan with \$4 earned each week.

deliverable: A chart with a goal, a price and four weeks of earn, save and spend, with the totals added up.
deliverable: A short talk, told to your grown-up, saying how close you came and what you would change.
rubric: The goal is named and its price is written, and the price is under \$20.
rubric: In each week, Save plus Spend (plus Give, if used) equals the Earn amount.
rubric: The Total saved column adds the Save amount each week and the numbers are correct.
rubric: The child says how much more money is needed, or says the goal was reached.
rubric: The child names at least one thing they would change or keep the same, with a reason.
safety: Use pretend money, or a few real coins that your grown-up chooses. Never share the family's income or bills, and never use a website to buy or sign up for anything. Your grown-up decides if any real money is spent on the goal.

tutor: How do I find how many more weeks I need to reach my goal?
tutor: How can I tell if my goal is a need or a want?

@review
### Week by week

1. **Coins and bills.** Penny 1¢, nickel 5¢, dime 10¢, quarter 25¢. Bills are \$1, \$5, \$10 and \$20. 100¢ is \$1.
2. **Counting.** Sort, biggest first, count on.
3. **Prices and change.** Add the prices. Count up from the price to find change.
4. **Better buy.** Find the price of one. The lower price for one is the better buy.
5. **Needs and wants.** Needs keep you safe and healthy. Pay for needs first.
6. **Earning.** Pay for work. Add or multiply the pay for each job.
7. **Saving.** Goal minus what you have is what you need. Divide by the weekly amount.
8. **Budget.** Save, spend and give jars add up to your money.
9. **Banks.** A deposit adds. A withdrawal subtracts. The result is the balance.
10. **Interest.** Extra money the bank pays. In our game, 1¢ for each whole dollar each week.
11. **Giving.** Share equally by dividing.
12. **Ads.** Ask: Do I need it? How much? Can I wait?

### Key ideas

- Goal - saved = still needed.
- Still needed / weekly saving = weeks.
- Change = money paid - price.

### Self-check

- I can name every coin and bill.
- I can count a mixed handful of coins.
- I can make change.
- I can explain a need and a want.
- I can make a plan with save, spend and give.

### Common mistakes

Counting a coin by its size, forgetting to subtract what you already have, and adding on a withdrawal.

@exam num
prompt: You earn \$4 each day for 3 days. You give \$2, spend \$3 and save all the rest. How many dollars do you save?
answer: 7
lead: \$
check: 4 * 3 - 2 - 3
hint: Find the total you earned first: 4 for each of 3 days.
hint: Take away what you gave and what you spent.
solution: You earn 4 x 3 = 12 dollars. You give 2 and spend 3, which is 5 dollars. 12 - 5 = 7. You save \$7.

@exam num
prompt: Your goal is \$19. You have saved \$7 and you save \$3 each week. How many weeks will it take to reach your goal?
answer: 4
unit: weeks
check: (19 - 7) // 3
hint: How much more do you need?
hint: You need 19 - 7 = 12 dollars. Share 12 into groups of 3.
solution: You need 19 - 7 = 12 dollars. Saving \$3 each week: 3, 6, 9, 12. It takes 4 weeks.
