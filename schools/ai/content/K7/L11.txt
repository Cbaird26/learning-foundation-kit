@lesson
title: My body and its parts
summary: Name body parts and tell what job each one does.
objective: Name parts such as head, arms, legs, hands, feet, eyes, ears, nose and mouth.
objective: Tell the job of the heart, lungs, brain and bones.
objective: Say that your body works as a team.
reading: NASA Space Place: look up "How do astronauts stay healthy?" for a look at body care, or ask your grown-up to read a picture book about the body.

@article
Your body has many parts. Each part has a job.

- Your **eyes** see. Your **ears** hear. Your **nose** smells.
- Your **mouth** tastes, talks and eats.
- Your **hands** hold things. Your **legs** and **feet** help you walk and run.

Inside your body there are parts you cannot see.

- Your **brain** is in your head. It helps you think and tells your body what to do.
- Your **heart** pumps blood around your body. Put your hand on your chest to feel it beat.
- Your **lungs** take in air when you breathe in. Put your hand on your belly and breathe slowly to feel it move.
- Your **bones** hold you up. They are called your skeleton.
- Your **muscles** help you move. They pull on your bones.
- Your **stomach** helps break down the food you eat.

### A team

Your body parts work together. When you catch a ball, your eyes see it, your brain decides, and your arms and hands move.

@example
title: Feel your heart
problem: Your grown-up asks you to jump 10 times and then feel your heart. What do you notice?
step: Put your hand on your chest before jumping. You feel a slow beat.
step: Jump 10 times, then feel again. The heart beats faster, because your muscles need more blood when you move.

@mistakes
- Thinking the heart is shaped like a valentine. A real heart is a strong muscle that pumps blood.
- Thinking you breathe with your stomach. Air goes into your lungs.

@practice num
prompt: You have 2 hands and each hand has 5 fingers. How many fingers do you have on both hands?
answer: 10
check: 2 * 5
hint: Count 5 fingers on one hand, then count 5 more on the other.
solution: 5 + 5 = 10 fingers.
