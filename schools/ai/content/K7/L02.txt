@lesson
title: Living and nonliving things
summary: Sort things into living and nonliving by asking what living things do.
objective: Say that living things grow, need food and water, and can have babies.
objective: Say that nonliving things do not grow or eat.
objective: Sort things as living or nonliving.
reading: NASA Space Place: look up "What is life?" or ask your grown-up to read a picture book about living things.

@article
A **living thing** is alive. Plants, animals and people are living things.

Living things:

- **grow** and change,
- **need food and water** (plants make their own food from sunlight),
- **can make babies** of their own kind,
- **need air.**

A **nonliving thing** is not alive. A rock, a spoon and a cloud are nonliving. They do not eat, grow or have babies.

Some things can move but are not alive. A toy car rolls when you push it. A river flows. They are nonliving, because they do not eat, grow or have babies.

Some things were once alive. A dry leaf on the ground or a wooden chair came from a plant. They are not alive now.

### A good question

Ask: does it grow, eat or drink, and have babies? If yes, it is living.

@example
title: Is a puppy living?
problem: Is a puppy living or nonliving? Is a stuffed toy puppy living or nonliving?
step: A real puppy grows bigger, drinks and eats, and can one day have puppies. So it is living.
step: A toy puppy never grows, eats or has puppies. So it is nonliving, even though it looks like a puppy.

@mistakes
- Thinking that anything that moves is alive. A toy car and a river move but are nonliving.
- Thinking a plant is nonliving because it does not walk. Plants grow and need water, so they are living.

@practice mc
prompt: Which of these is a living thing?
choice: A rock
choice*: A tree
choice: A spoon
choice: A cloud
hint: Which one grows bigger and needs water?
solution: A tree grows and needs water and sunlight, so it is living.

@practice mc
prompt: Which of these is nonliving?
choice: A cat
choice: A daisy
choice*: A pencil
choice: A bird
hint: Which one never eats, drinks or grows?
solution: A pencil does not eat, grow or have babies, so it is nonliving.

@practice num
prompt: A garden has 4 flowers and 3 rocks. How many living things are in the garden?
answer: 4
check: 4
hint: Flowers grow. Rocks do not. Count only the flowers.
solution: The 4 flowers are living. The rocks are nonliving. So the answer is 4.

@practice num
prompt: A tray has 2 birds, 1 ball, 3 ants and 2 shoes. How many living things are on the tray?
answer: 5
check: 2 + 3
hint: Birds and ants are animals. Balls and shoes are not alive.
solution: Birds and ants are living: 2 + 3 = 5.

@practice self
prompt: Look around a room with your grown-up. Name two living things and two nonliving things. Tell one reason that one living thing is alive.
solution: Living: a houseplant and the family cat. Nonliving: a chair and a cup. The plant is alive because it grows and needs water.
rubric: You named two living things and two nonliving things.
rubric: Your reason says something living things do, such as grow, eat or drink, or have babies.
hint: Think about which things in the room need water or food. Those are the living ones.

@quiz mc
prompt: A toy robot walks across the table. Is it living?
choice: Yes, because it moves
choice*: No, because it does not eat, grow or have babies
choice: Yes, because it has a face
choice: Yes, because it makes noise
hint: Remember the questions: does it grow, eat or drink, and have babies?
solution: The robot moves, but it does not eat, grow or have babies. It is nonliving.

@quiz num
prompt: A pond has 3 fish, 2 frogs and 5 stones. How many living things are in the pond?
answer: 5
check: 3 + 2
hint: Fish and frogs are animals. Stones are not alive.
solution: 3 + 2 = 5 living things.
