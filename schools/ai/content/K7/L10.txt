@lesson
title: Land and water on Earth
summary: Tell land forms and bodies of water apart and find land and water on a map or globe.
objective: Name land forms such as mountains, hills and valleys.
objective: Name bodies of water such as oceans, lakes, rivers and ponds.
objective: Find land and water on a map or globe.
reading: NASA Space Place: look up "What is Earth?" or "Why is Earth called the Blue Planet?" and ask your grown-up to read it with you.

@article
Earth has land and water. On a globe, the blue parts are water and the green and brown parts are land.

### Land

- A **mountain** is very high land.
- A **hill** is lower than a mountain.
- A **valley** is low land between hills or mountains.
- An **island** is land with water all around it.

### Water

- An **ocean** is very big and salty. Earth has a lot of ocean.
- A **lake** has land all around it. A **pond** is a small lake.
- A **river** is water that flows. It runs from high land down to a lake or ocean.

More of Earth is covered by water than by land. A **map** is a flat picture of a place. Maps use blue for water and green or brown for land.

### Try it

With your grown-up, look at a globe or a map. Point to something blue and something green or brown.

@example
title: Land or water on a map
problem: On a map, a big blue part has a small green part in the middle. What is the small green part?
step: Blue is water. The green part is land.
step: Land with water all around it is an island.

@mistakes
- Thinking all water is the same. Oceans are salty, and most lakes and rivers are not.
- Mixing up a lake and an island. A lake is water with land around it. An island is land with water around it.

@quiz num

check: 6 - 2
hint: Take 2 away from 6.
