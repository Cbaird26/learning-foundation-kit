@article
In the day we see the **Sun**. The Sun is a star. It gives us light and makes us warm. Never look straight at the Sun. It can hurt your eyes.

At night we see the **Moon** and **stars**. The stars are far away. They look small, but they are giant balls of hot gas, like our Sun.

### Day and night

Earth spins like a slow top. When your part of Earth faces the Sun, it is day. When it turns away, it is night. One spin takes one day.

### The Moon

The Moon does not make its own light. It shines because sunlight bounces off it. Its shape seems to change each night. Sometimes it is a thin curve, sometimes half, sometimes a full circle, and sometimes we cannot see it. The shape changes in a pattern that takes about a month.

### Moon drawing

With your grown-up, look at the Moon on clear nights. Draw its shape each night you can see it. Draw the date next to it.

@example
title: Why is it dark at night?
problem: Why is it dark at night?
step: Earth spins. At night your part of Earth faces away from the Sun.
step: So the sunlight does not reach you. The sky is dark, and we can see the Moon and the stars.

@mistakes
- Thinking the Moon makes its own light. It shines because the Sun lights it.
- Looking straight at the Sun. Never do this.
