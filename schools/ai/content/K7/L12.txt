@lesson
title: Healthy habits
summary: Tell the habits that keep your body healthy and plan a healthy day.
objective: Name healthy habits: washing hands, eating well, sleeping, playing and brushing teeth.
objective: Tell why each habit helps your body.
objective: Make a healthy-day chart.
reading: NASA Space Place: look up "How do astronauts stay healthy?" or ask your grown-up to read a picture book about healthy habits.

@article
A **habit** is something you do again and again. **Healthy habits** keep your body strong.

- **Wash your hands** with soap and water for as long as it takes to sing your ABCs. This washes away germs.
- **Eat well.** Fruits, vegetables, grains and proteins give your body energy. Sweets are for sometimes.
- **Drink water** to keep your body working.
- **Sleep.** Children need a lot of sleep to grow and learn: about 10 to 13 hours a day (naps count) at age 5, and 9 to 12 hours a night at ages 6 to 12.
- **Move and play** every day to make your muscles, heart and bones strong.
- **Brush your teeth** two times a day.
- **Stay safe.** Wear a helmet on a bike. Ask a grown-up before you take any medicine.

### Healthy-day chart

Make a chart with your grown-up. Draw a box for each habit. Each night, color in the boxes for the habits you did that day.

@example
title: A healthy day
problem: Plan a healthy morning with 3 habits.
step: Wake up and brush your teeth. This keeps your teeth clean.
step: Eat a breakfast with fruit, and drink a glass of water.
step: Before lunch, wash your hands with soap. So there are 3 habits: brushing, eating well and washing.

@mistakes
- Thinking you only need to wash your hands when they look dirty. Germs are too small to see.
- Skipping sleep to play. Your body grows and rests while you sleep.

@practice num
prompt: You brush your teeth 2 times each day. How many times do you brush in 3 days?
answer: 6
check: 2 * 3
hint: Count by twos: 2, 4, and then one more.
solution: 2 + 2 + 2 = 6 times.

@practice num
prompt: Your healthy-day chart has 6 boxes. You color 4 boxes. How many boxes are left to color?
answer: 2
check: 6 - 4
hint: Start at 6 and count back 4.
solution: 6 - 4 = 2 boxes.
