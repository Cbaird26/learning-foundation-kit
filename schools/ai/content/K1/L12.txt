@article
A clock helps us know what time it is.

### Two hands

A clock has two hands. The short hand is the **hour hand**. It tells the hour. The long hand is the **minute hand**. It tells the minutes.

### On the hour

When the long minute hand points straight up at the 12, it is exactly on the hour. Then look at the short hour hand. If it points to the 3, the time is **3 o'clock**. We write 3:00.

### On the half hour

When the minute hand points straight down at the 6, half an hour has gone by. It is **half past** the hour. If the hour hand is halfway between the 3 and the 4, the time is **half past 3**. We write 3:30.

### Say it two ways

- 7:00 is "7 o'clock".
- 7:30 is "half past 7".

### Times of your day

Think of things you do. You might wake up at 7:00, eat lunch at 12:00, and go to bed at 8:00. Look at a real clock with your grown-up at different times of day.

### Practice

Pick a clock in your home. Ask your grown-up to move the hands. Say the time each time.

@example
title: Read the clock
problem: The minute hand points to the 12. The hour hand points to the 5. What time is it?
step: The minute hand on the 12 means it is on the hour.
step: The hour hand is on the 5, so it is 5 o'clock.
step: We write 5:00.

@example
title: Half past
problem: The minute hand points to the 6. The hour hand is between the 8 and the 9. What time is it?
step: The minute hand on the 6 means half past.
step: The hour hand has gone past the 8 but is not at the 9 yet, so the hour is 8.
step: The time is half past 8, or 8:30.

@mistakes
- Mixing up the short hour hand and the long minute hand.
- Reading half past 8 as 9:30 because the hour hand is close to the 9.
- Writing 3:00 for a time that should be 3:30.
