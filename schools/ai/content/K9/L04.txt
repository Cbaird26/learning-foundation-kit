@article
A **landform** is a natural shape on the land. Here are some:

- A **mountain** is very high land with a peak.
- A **hill** is a smaller, rounded bump of land.
- A **valley** is low land between hills or mountains.
- A **plain** is wide, flat land.
- An **island** is land with water all around it.
- A **peninsula** is land with water on three sides.
- A **desert** is very dry land.

Bodies of water are shaped by the land too. A **river** is a long stream of water that flows. A **lake** has land all around it.

### The seven continents

A **continent** is a very big area of land. There are seven:

1. Africa
2. Antarctica
3. Asia
4. Australia (sometimes called Oceania)
5. Europe
6. North America
7. South America

Asia is the biggest continent in size and in number of people. Antarctica is very cold and covered in ice. People visit it to do science, but no one lives there all year.

### The five oceans

An **ocean** is a very big body of salt water. The five oceans are the Pacific, the Atlantic, the Indian, the Arctic and the Southern. The Pacific Ocean is the biggest. Most of the Earth's surface is covered with water.

### Try this

With a grown-up, look at a world map or a globe. Put your finger on the continent where you live. Then find the ocean closest to you.

@example
title: Find the landform
problem: A piece of land has water on all sides. What landform is it?
step: Count the sides with water.
step: Water is on every side.
step: This is an island.

@example
title: Count the continents
problem: You have named North America, South America and Africa. How many continents are left to name?
step: There are 7 continents in all.
step: You have named 3.
step: 7 - 3 = 4 continents are left.

@mistakes
- Mixing up a continent and a country. A continent is huge and holds many countries.
- Thinking there are only four oceans. There are five.
- Mixing up an island (water on all sides) and a peninsula (water on three sides).
