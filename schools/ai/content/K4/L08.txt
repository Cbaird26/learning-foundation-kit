@article
You know the short vowels. Now meet the **long vowels**. A long vowel says its **name**: a says "ay", e says "ee", i says "eye", o says "oh", u says "you".

### The silent e

When a word ends in **e**, the e stays quiet. But it does a job: it makes the vowel before it say its name. We sometimes call it the "magic e".

- cap becomes cape
- kit becomes kite
- hop becomes hope
- cub becomes cube
- tub becomes tube

### How to read it

Look at the end of the word. If you see a vowel, then a consonant, then e, say the vowel's name. For "cake": c, a (says "ay"), k, e is silent. Cake.

### Try it with your grown-up

Write cap on a card. Say it. Then add an e to make cape. Say it. Do the same with kit and kite. Notice how the sound of the vowel changes.

### More silent e words

Here are more to read: make, bike, home, game, nose, five, cute, rope.

@example
title: Add the silent e
problem: Add an e to the word "mad". What new word do you get?
step: Read the word you have: mad, with a short a.
step: Add e at the end: m-a-d-e.
step: The e is silent, and it makes a say its name: "made".

@mistakes
- Reading the e at the end. It is silent.
- Forgetting to say the vowel's name. In "bike", i says "eye".
- Adding an e to every word. Only some words end in silent e.

@practice num
prompt: How many of these words end in a silent e: cake, cat, bike, dog, home?
answer: 3
check: sum(w.endswith("e") for w in ["cake", "cat", "bike", "dog", "home"])
hint: Look at the last letter of each word.
solution: Cake, bike and home end in e. Cat and dog do not. That makes 3.

@practice num

check: len("bike")
hint: Point to b, i, k, e and count.
solution: Bike has 4 letters: b, i, k, e. The e is silent, so you say 3 sounds.
