# Med School Learning Foundation

**Open the school:** https://cbaird26.github.io/med-school-learning-foundation/

A Khan Academy-style self-study school: 74 courses in seven stages, from kindergarten to medical school and the USMLE —
elementary, middle and high school, college pre-medical courses, medical foundations, organ systems, and clerkships with
board review.

Each course has 12 weekly lessons (an article, worked examples, common mistakes, and auto-graded practice and quiz
questions), a week 13–14 project with a review guide, and a cumulative final exam. Skills move through mastery levels
with unit quizzes, a midterm and course challenges, and missed questions go into an error log that brings back a
parallel problem 48 hours later.

## Using it

Open the site in any modern browser. The whole school is one self-contained page (`index.html`, about 10 MB), so you can also save the page and use it offline.

Progress and the error log are kept only in your own browser and are never uploaded. Keep using the same browser and do not clear its site data; another browser or device starts fresh. The Tutor button prepares a prompt you can copy into a chat with Claude.

## Accuracy

Every course was written and then independently reviewed. Medical content follows 2025 teaching and notes later
guideline changes (for example the 2024 hyperglycemic-crisis consensus, the 2026 ACC/AHA dyslipidemia guideline and the
December 2025 U.S. withdrawal of andexanet alfa). Medical content is for education only, not medical advice; check any
dose or guideline against current references before clinical use. Self-study scores do not award credit.
