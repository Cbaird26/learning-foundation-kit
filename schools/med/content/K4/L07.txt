@article
Some words show up again and again in books. You will read them so often that you should know them **by sight**, fast, without sounding out. These are called **sight words**.

### Easy ones and tricky ones

Many sight words you can sound out, like "and", "it", "in", "on" and "can". Some are **tricky** because they do not sound the way they are spelled.

- the: the e does not say its usual sound.
- was: the a says /u/ and the s says /z/.
- said: it sounds like "sed".
- of: it sounds like "uv".
- you, to, do: the spelling is unusual.

### Words to learn

Here are 30 common words to practice over time: the, a, I, and, is, it, in, to, you, he, she, we, my, me, was, said, are, of, have, like, see, go, no, so, for, on, can, up, look, come.

### How to practice

Make word cards. Show a card. If you read it fast, put it in the "got it" pile. If you need to think, put it in the "again" pile. Practice the "again" pile a little each day.

### Try it with your grown-up

Open a picture book. Find the word "the" five times. Then find "and" and "is".

@example
title: Spot the sight words
problem: In "I see a cat and a dog", which words are on this list: I, a, and?
step: Read the sentence slowly: I - see - a - cat - and - a - dog.
step: Check each word against the list. I, a, and, a are on it.
step: There are 4 list words in the sentence. See, cat and dog are not on the list.

@mistakes
- Sounding out tricky words like "said" as "sayd". Learn them by heart.
- Guessing a word from its first letter. Look at the whole word.
- Practicing too many new words at once. Learn a few at a time.

@practice num
prompt: Look at this list: the, is, and. How many of those words are in the sentence "The dog is big and the cat is small."?
answer: 5
check: sum(w.strip(".").lower() in ["the", "is", "and"] for w in "The dog is big and the cat is small.".split())
hint: Read the sentence one word at a time and check each word against the list.
hint: The shows up twice and is shows up twice. Count and too.
solution: The, is, and, the, is: that is 5 words from the list.

@practice num
prompt: How many words are in the sentence "I like to go up"?
answer: 5
check: len("I like to go up".split())
hint: Point to each word and count it.
solution: I, like, to, go, up is 5 words.

@quiz num
prompt: Look at this list: you, my, we. How many of those words are in the sentence "You and I can see my cat, and we can go."?
answer: 3
check: sum(w.strip(",.").lower() in ["you", "my", "we"] for w in "You and I can see my cat, and we can go.".split())
hint: Read one word at a time and check it against the list.
solution: You, my and we are in the sentence. That makes 3.
