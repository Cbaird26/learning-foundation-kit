objective: Answer who, what, where and why questions from the words in a story.

reading: Reading Rockets, "Phonics and decoding": look up the topics "retelling" and "comprehension questions".

@article
When you **retell** a story, you tell it again in your own words. You tell the important parts, **in order**.

### Words that help

Use order words: **first**, **next**, **then**, **last**. They keep the story in the right order.

### A small story

Read this with your grown-up.

Tia had a seed. First, she put it in a pot with soil. Next, she gave it water. Then she put the pot by the window. After many days, a green sprout came up. Tia clapped.

A retelling: First, Tia put a seed in soil. Next, she watered it. Then she put it by the window. Last, a sprout came up.

### Answering questions

Story questions often start with a word.

- **Who** asks about a character: Who had a seed? Tia.
- **What** asks about an event: What came up? A sprout.
- **Where** asks about the place: Where did she put the pot? By the window.
- **Why** asks for a reason: Why did Tia clap? Because the sprout came up.

If you are not sure, go back and find the answer in the story. Put your finger on the words that tell you.

### Try it with your grown-up

After you read any short story, each of you retells it using first, next and last. Then ask each other one who question and one why question.

@example
title: Put events in order
problem: In the story about Tia, what did she do right after she put the seed in the soil?
step: Find the order words. First: seed in the pot with soil.
step: The next order word is "next": she gave it water.
step: So right after the soil, Tia watered the seed.

@mistakes
- Retelling in the wrong order. Use first, next and last.
- Telling every tiny detail. Tell the big events.
- Answering from memory without checking the story. Look back at the words.

@practice mc
prompt: Read: "Dan fed his fish. Then he cleaned the tank. Last, he said good night to the fish." What did Dan do last?
choice: He fed his fish.
choice: He cleaned the tank.
choice*: He said good night to the fish.
choice: He bought a fish.
hint: Look for the word "last".
solution: The word "last" tells us Dan said good night to the fish.

@practice mc
prompt: Read: "Mom baked a pie. It was hot, so she put it on the sill. Then the dog came." Why did Mom put the pie on the sill?
choice: She wanted the dog to eat it.
choice*: The pie was hot.
choice: She was done.
choice: It was night.
hint: Find the word "so". The reason comes just before it.
solution: The story says the pie was hot, so Mom put it on the sill.

check: len(["got a cup", "got milk", "poured it", "drank it"])
hint: Count each event after the order words first, next, then and last.
solution: Ann got a cup, got milk, poured it, and drank it. That is 4 things.
