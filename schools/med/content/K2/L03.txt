@article
### Coins

Each coin has a value in cents.

| Coin | Value |
|---|---|
| penny | 1 cent |
| nickel | 5 cents |
| dime | 10 cents |
| quarter | 25 cents |

One dollar is 100 cents. To count coins, start with the biggest coin and skip count, then add the smaller ones. For 1 quarter, 2 dimes and 1 nickel say: 25, 35, 45, 50. That is 50 cents.

### Making change

Change is the money you get back. If a toy costs 60 cents and you pay with \$1 (100 cents), count up from 60 to 100. 60 to 70 is a dime, then 70 to 100 is 30 more. You can also subtract: $100 - 60 = 40$. Your change is 40 cents.

### Telling time

A clock has two hands. The short hand is the **hour hand**. The long hand is the **minute hand**.

The minute hand goes all the way around in 60 minutes. Each number on the clock is 5 minutes more than the one before it. So skip count by 5 as you move around the clock: 12 is 0, 1 is 5, 2 is 10, 3 is 15, 4 is 20, 5 is 25, 6 is 30, and so on.

- If the minute hand is on 3, it is 15 minutes after the hour: a quarter past.
- If the minute hand is on 6, it is 30 minutes after the hour: half past.
- If the minute hand is on 9, it is 45 minutes after the hour.

Say the hour that the hour hand has just passed. If the hour hand is a little past 4 and the minute hand is on 3, the time is 4:15.

@example
title: Count a handful of coins
problem: You have 1 quarter, 2 dimes and 1 nickel. How many cents is that?
step: Start with the quarter: 25 cents.
step: Skip count by 10 for each dime: 35, then 45.
step: Add the nickel: 50 cents.

@example
title: Read a clock
problem: The hour hand is just past the 4. The minute hand points to the 3. What time is it?
step: The minute hand is on 3. Count by 5s: 5, 10, 15. That is 15 minutes after.
step: The hour hand has just passed 4, so the hour is 4. The time is 4:15.

@mistakes
- Counting every coin as 1, such as treating a dime as 1 cent.
- Reading the minute hand as 3 minutes instead of counting by 5s to get 15.
- Using the next hour instead of the hour the short hand has just passed.
