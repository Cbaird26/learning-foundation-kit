@article
You know the vowels: a, e, i, o and u. Sometimes two vowels sit side by side and work together as a **vowel team**. The team makes one sound.

### Vowel teams

Look at these words. Say each one out loud.

- **ai** and **ay** say long a: rain, play.
- **ee** and **ea** say long e: feet, beat.
- **oa** and **ow** can say long o: boat, snow.
- **oo** can say the sound in moon or the sound in book.
- **ou** and **ow** can say the sound in out and cow.
- **oi** and **oy** say the sound in coin and toy.

A tip: **ay** and **oy** like to come at the end of a word (play, boy). **ai** and **oi** like to come in the middle (rain, coin).

### R-controlled vowels

The letter **r** is bossy. When r comes right after a vowel, it changes the vowel sound.

- **ar** says "ar" as in car and star.
- **or** says "or" as in corn and fork.
- **er**, **ir** and **ur** all say the same sound, "er", as in fern, bird and turn.

Because er, ir and ur sound the same, you have to remember how each word is spelled. Looking at words in books helps your eyes remember.

### How to read a new word

First, look for a vowel team or an r-controlled vowel. Circle it in your mind. Then say the sound of the team, and blend the other letters around it. Try it with your grown-up!

@example
title: Reading the word "boat"
problem: How do you read the word boat?
step: Look for the vowel team. The letters o and a sit side by side. The team is oa.
step: The team oa says long o, the sound you hear in "go".
step: Blend the sounds: b, oa, t. The word is boat.

@example
title: Sorting er, ir and ur words
problem: The words fern, bird and turn all sound like they have the same middle. What is the same?
step: Underline the vowel and r in each word: fern, bird, turn.
step: The spellings are different: er, ir and ur.
step: But all three say the same sound, "er". They are r-controlled vowels.

@mistakes
- Reading both vowels in a team separately, like "r-a-i-n" with two sounds, instead of one sound for ai.
- Forgetting that r changes the vowel, so reading "car" with the short a from "cat".
- Thinking er, ir and ur sound different from each other. They sound the same, so check the spelling in a book.
