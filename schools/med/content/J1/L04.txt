objective: Place the decimal point correctly when multiplying and dividing.

@article
Decimals are fractions with denominators $10$, $100$, $1000$ and so on. Each place is ten times the place to its right, so the same rules you use for whole numbers work, as long as you track the decimal point.

### Adding and subtracting

**Line up the decimal points**, and fill empty places with zeros so the columns match. For $12.4 - 5.87$, write $12.40 - 5.87 = 6.53$.

### Multiplying

Multiply as if there were no decimal points. Then count the decimal places in both factors together and give the product that many.

For $2.4 \times 0.35$: $24 \times 35 = 840$. The factors have $1 + 2 = 3$ decimal places, so the product is $0.840 = 0.84$.

### Dividing

To divide by a decimal, make the divisor a whole number by multiplying **both** numbers by the same power of $10$. This keeps the quotient the same, just like an equivalent ratio.

For $4.5 \div 0.15$, multiply both by $100$ to get $450 \div 15 = 30$.

### Estimate first

Round to friendly numbers to see about how big the answer should be. For $4.98 \times 3.02$, think $5 \times 3 = 15$. If you get $1.5$ or $150$, the decimal point is misplaced.

### Check division with multiplication

Division is undone by multiplication, so $\text{quotient} \times \text{divisor}$ should equal the dividend. This works for fractions too: if $\frac{3}{4} \div \frac{1}{2} = \frac{3}{2}$, then $\frac{3}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{3}{4}$ confirms it. Use the check whenever the decimal or fraction work gets long.

### Money and measurement

Decimals appear whenever you use dollars or metric units. With money, keep two decimal places in your final answer. When a problem asks to round, say to the nearest hundredth, look at the digit after the place you are keeping.

@example
title: Dividing by a decimal
problem: Compute $4.5 \div 0.15$ and check your answer.
step: The divisor $0.15$ has two decimal places, so multiply both numbers by $100$: $4.5 \times 100 = 450$ and $0.15 \times 100 = 15$.
step: Divide the whole numbers: $450 \div 15 = 30$.
step: Check with multiplication: $30 \times 0.15 = 4.5$. This matches the dividend, so the quotient is $30$.

@mistakes
- Not lining up decimal points when adding or subtracting, such as adding $3.5 + 0.25$ as $3.5 + 25$.
- Placing the decimal point in a product by lining up the factors instead of counting the decimal places.
- Multiplying only the divisor by a power of $10$ and not the dividend.
