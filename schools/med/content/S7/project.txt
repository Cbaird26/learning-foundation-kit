### Week by week

**Week 1.** Hematopoiesis runs from the stem cell to progenitors under EPO, TPO and CSFs. Indices: $\text{MCV} = \text{Hct} \times 10 / \text{RBC}$, MCHC = Hb/Hct. Corrected reticulocyte count = retic percent times Hct/45; RPI divides again by a maturation factor, and RPI above 2 means appropriate marrow response.

**Week 2.** Microcytic (TAILS): iron deficiency (low ferritin, high TIBC, high RDW), thalassemia (normal iron, high RBC, Mentzer below 13, raised HbA2), inflammation (high ferritin, low TIBC, hepcidin). Iron deficiency in a man or postmenopausal woman needs GI evaluation.

**Week 3.** B12 and folate: both raise homocysteine, only B12 raises MMA and causes neurologic disease. Never give folate alone for undiagnosed B12 deficiency. Non-megaloblastic macrocytosis: alcohol, liver, thyroid. Aplastic anemia: hypocellular marrow, treat with transplant or immunosuppression.

**Week 4.** Confirm hemolysis (retic, LDH, haptoglobin, indirect bilirubin), then DAT. Spherocytosis (high MCHC, EMA), G6PD (Heinz and bite cells), warm and cold AIHA, PNH (CD55 and CD59), sickle cell disease (HbS, hydroxyurea, infection risk).

**Week 5.** PT and INR measure the extrinsic and common pathways; aPTT measures the intrinsic and common. Mixing corrects in deficiency, not with an inhibitor. Hemophilia, vWD, ITP, TTP (plasma exchange, no platelets), DIC, vitamin K deficiency.

**Week 6.** Virchow triad, factor V Leiden, antiphospholipid syndrome, DVT and PE workup, heparin (aPTT, protamine, HIT), warfarin (INR, vitamin K, PCC), DOACs (idarucizumab for dabigatran, 4-factor PCC for factor Xa inhibitors).

**Week 7.** ABO and Rh compatibility, products and thresholds, and the transfusion reactions: hemolytic, febrile, allergic, anaphylactic, TRALI versus TACO, delayed, GVHD.

**Week 8.** ANC; AML (Auer rods, 7+3), APL (ATRA at once), ALL, CML (BCR-ABL, imatinib), CLL (smudge cells), PV, ET, myelofibrosis.

**Week 9.** Hodgkin (Reed-Sternberg, contiguous, ABVD) versus non-Hodgkin (R-CHOP; key translocations), myeloma (CRAB, M protein), MGUS, Waldenstrom.

**Week 10.** Oncogenes versus tumor suppressors, carcinogens, grade versus stage, markers, screening and its biases, paraneoplastic syndromes.

**Week 11.** Drug and toxicity pairs, BSA, Calvert, mesna, leucovorin, dexrazoxane, checkpoint inhibitor toxicity.

**Week 12.** TLS (rasburicase after G6PD check), spinal cord compression (dexamethasone and emergent MRI), febrile neutropenia (antibiotics within 60 minutes), hypercalcemia (saline first), SVC syndrome, leukostasis.

### Common errors

Treating ferritin as reliable in inflammation; forgetting that the INR is for warfarin; transfusing platelets in TTP or HIT; waiting for confirmation in APL; skipping the DAT; giving diuretics for TRALI; and mistaking stage for grade.
