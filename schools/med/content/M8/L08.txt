@article
The immune system can injure its host when it responds excessively, against harmless antigens, or against self. **Immunopathology** classifies this injury, and **amyloidosis** is a related disorder of protein folding in which a misfolded protein deposits in tissue.

### Hypersensitivity

| Type | Mechanism | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **I** (immediate) | IgE bound to mast cells; antigen cross-links, releasing histamine and leukotrienes within minutes; late phase hours later | Anaphylaxis, allergic rhinitis, asthma, food allergy |
| **II** (antibody against cells or matrix) | IgG or IgM binds cell-surface or matrix antigen; complement, opsonization or receptor dysfunction | Autoimmune hemolytic anemia, **Goodpasture syndrome** (anti-GBM, linear immunofluorescence), rheumatic fever, **myasthenia gravis** (blocking acetylcholine receptors), Graves disease (stimulating TSH receptor), hyperacute rejection |
| **III** (immune complex) | Antigen-antibody complexes deposit in vessels, activate complement; neutrophils injure tissue | **SLE**, serum sickness, poststreptococcal glomerulonephritis (granular immunofluorescence), polyarteritis nodosa |
| **IV** (delayed, T cell) | Sensitized CD4+ Th1 cells activate macrophages, or CD8+ T cells kill directly; peaks 48 to 72 hours | Contact dermatitis, tuberculin skin test, type 1 diabetes, multiple sclerosis, granulomas |

### Autoimmunity

Self-tolerance is maintained by **central tolerance** (clonal deletion of self-reactive cells in thymus and marrow) and **peripheral tolerance** (anergy, regulatory T cells, deletion). Failure, with genetic factors (HLA associations) and environmental triggers such as infection, leads to autoimmunity.

- **SLE:** ANA is sensitive; anti-double-stranded DNA and **anti-Smith** are specific; immune complex glomerulonephritis, malar rash, arthritis, serositis, cytopenias; low C3 and C4 in active disease.
- **Sjögren syndrome:** anti-SSA (Ro) and anti-SSB (La); dry eyes and mouth.
- **Systemic sclerosis:** anti-topoisomerase I (**anti-Scl-70**) in diffuse disease; **anticentromere** antibodies in limited disease (CREST).
- **Rheumatoid arthritis:** rheumatoid factor and anti-CCP (more specific).
- **Primary biliary cholangitis:** antimitochondrial antibody.

### Transplant rejection

**Hyperacute** rejection occurs within minutes to hours from preformed antibodies (type II) and causes thrombosis; **acute cellular** rejection (days to months) involves T cells attacking graft cells and vessels; **acute humoral** rejection involves antibodies and vasculitis; **chronic** rejection (months to years) shows vascular intimal fibrosis (graft arteriosclerosis) and, in the lung, obliterative bronchiolitis. **Graft-versus-host disease** occurs when donor T cells attack the recipient, classically after bone marrow transplant, with skin, liver (bile duct) and gut involvement.

### Immunodeficiency in brief

X-linked (Bruton) agammaglobulinemia (BTK; absent B cells; recurrent bacterial infections after maternal IgG wanes), selective IgA deficiency (the most common; anaphylaxis with transfusion), DiGeorge (T cell), severe combined immunodeficiency, and HIV (CD4+ T cell depletion).

### Amyloidosis

**Amyloid** is an extracellular deposit of proteins that have misfolded into **beta-pleated sheet** fibrils. On H&E it is an amorphous pink hyaline material. With **Congo red** stain it appears pink to red, and under polarized light shows **apple-green birefringence**. Deposits damage tissue by displacing cells and impairing function.

| Type | Precursor | Setting | Main organs |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AL** | Immunoglobulin light chains | Plasma cell dyscrasia, multiple myeloma | Heart (restrictive cardiomyopathy), kidney (nephrotic syndrome), tongue, nerve, liver |
| **AA** | Serum amyloid A (acute-phase protein) | Chronic inflammation: rheumatoid arthritis, inflammatory bowel disease, familial Mediterranean fever, chronic infection | Kidney (first and most important), liver, spleen |
| **ATTR** | Transthyretin | Wild-type in elderly (senile cardiac); hereditary mutations | Heart; peripheral nerves |
| **A-beta2m** | Beta-2 microglobulin | Long-term dialysis | Joints, carpal tunnel |
| **A-beta** | Amyloid precursor protein | Alzheimer disease (plaques; APP gene on chromosome 21, so Down syndrome) | Brain |
| Others | Islet amyloid polypeptide; calcitonin; atrial natriuretic peptide | Type 2 diabetes; medullary thyroid carcinoma; aging atrium | Pancreatic islets; thyroid; heart |

Clinical clues: nephrotic syndrome with enlarged kidneys; restrictive cardiomyopathy with thick walls on echo but low QRS voltage on ECG; macroglossia; carpal tunnel; hepatosplenomegaly. Diagnosis: biopsy (abdominal fat pad aspirate, rectal or involved organ) with Congo red; typing by immunohistochemistry or mass spectrometry; serum and urine protein electrophoresis with immunofixation and serum free light chains when AL is suspected.

@example
title: Choosing the amyloid type from the clinical setting
problem: A 62-year-old man with long-standing rheumatoid arthritis develops proteinuria of 6 g/day and edema. A kidney biopsy shows amorphous pink material in glomeruli that stains with Congo red and shows apple-green birefringence. Which amyloid type is present, and why?
step: The stain confirms amyloid. The setting is chronic inflammation.
step: Chronic inflammation raises IL-6 and the liver's production of the acute-phase protein serum amyloid A, which is cleaved and deposits as AA amyloid.
step: AA amyloid affects kidney first, causing proteinuria and nephrotic syndrome; liver and spleen follow. AL amyloid would require a plasma cell neoplasm and favor heart and nerve.

@mistakes
- Labeling Goodpasture and myasthenia gravis as type III reactions; both are type II (antibody against fixed antigen).
- Assuming that delayed hypersensitivity involves antibodies; it is T-cell mediated.
- Expecting a positive Congo red to be yellow-green: apple-green birefringence under polarized light is the criterion.
- Forgetting AL amyloid occurs without myeloma in many patients (primary AL).

answer: 640
check: 40 * 2 ** (5 - 1)
tol: 0.5

solution: Tube 5 is 40 doubled four times: 40, 80, 160, 320, 640, so the titer is 1:640.
