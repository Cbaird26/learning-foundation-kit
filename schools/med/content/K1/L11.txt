@article
**Length** tells how long something is from one end to the other.

### Longer and shorter

Hold two pencils side by side with the bottoms lined up. The one that sticks out more is **longer**. The other is **shorter**. For things that stand up, like a tree, we say **taller** and **shorter**.

### Measure with paper clips

You can measure with small things that are all the same size, like paper clips or cubes. These are called **units**.

1. Line up the first paper clip at the start of the object.
2. Put the next paper clip right after it, end to end.
3. Leave no gaps and no overlaps.
4. Count the paper clips.

If 6 paper clips fit along a toy car, the car is **6 paper clips long**.

### Use the same unit

Measure two things with the same kind of unit. If you measure one thing with paper clips and another with cubes, you cannot compare them. Use the same unit for both.

### Compare

A crayon is 5 paper clips long. A marker is 8 paper clips long. The number 8 is more than 5, so the marker is longer.

@example
title: How long is the toy?
problem: You line up paper clips along a toy. 7 paper clips fit from one end to the other with no gaps. How long is the toy?
step: Each paper clip is one unit. Count them: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7.
step: The toy is 7 paper clips long.

@mistakes
- Leaving gaps between the paper clips, or not starting at the end of the object.
- Measuring two things with different units and then comparing the numbers.
- Starting to count at 0 instead of 1 for the first paper clip.

@practice num
prompt: A book is 9 paper clips long. A pencil is 6 paper clips long. How many paper clips long is the longer one?
answer: 9
check: max(9, 6)
hint: Which is more, 9 or 6?
solution: 9 is more than 6, so the book is longer. It is 9 paper clips long.

@practice num
prompt: A shoe is 8 paper clips long. A boot is 3 paper clips longer than the shoe. How many paper clips long is the boot?
answer: 11
check: 8 + 3
hint: Longer by 3 means add 3.
solution: $8 + 3 = 11$. The boot is 11 paper clips long.

solution: $7 + 2 = 9$. The fork is 9 cubes long.
