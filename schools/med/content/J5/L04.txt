@article
A single cell can do everything it needs to live, but a multicellular body does better by **specializing**. Different cells do different jobs, and groups of cells cooperate at higher levels of organization.

### Levels of organization

$$\text{cell} \to \text{tissue} \to \text{organ} \to \text{organ system} \to \text{organism}$$

- A **tissue** is a group of similar cells doing one job together.
- An **organ** is made of two or more tissues working together. The stomach is an organ.
- An **organ system** is a group of organs that cooperate. The stomach, intestines and liver belong to the digestive system.

### Four types of tissue in animals

1. **Epithelial tissue** covers surfaces and lines tubes, like the skin and the inside of the intestine.
2. **Connective tissue** supports and joins parts: bone, cartilage, fat and blood.
3. **Muscle tissue** contracts to cause movement.
4. **Nervous tissue** carries electrical signals.

The heart shows how this works. It is an organ with muscle tissue that squeezes, connective tissue that gives it strength and shape, epithelial tissue lining its chambers, and nervous tissue that helps control its rhythm.

### Organ systems and their jobs

| System | Main job |
|---|---|
| Digestive | Breaks down food and absorbs nutrients |
| Respiratory | Brings in oxygen and removes carbon dioxide |
| Circulatory | Moves blood to carry materials |
| Nervous | Sends signals and processes information |
| Skeletal | Supports the body and protects organs |
| Muscular | Moves the body |
| Excretory | Removes wastes |
| Immune | Defends against disease |

Systems rarely work alone. Muscles need oxygen from the respiratory system and the circulatory system to deliver it.

### Homeostasis

**Homeostasis** is keeping conditions inside the body in a steady range, such as a temperature near 37 °C (98.6 °F). It works by **negative feedback**: a change triggers a response that reverses the change. When you get too warm, you sweat and blood vessels in the skin widen, which cools you. When you are too cold, you shiver, which makes heat.

To convert Celsius to Fahrenheit, use

$$F = \tfrac{9}{5}C + 32.$$

@example
title: Following a negative feedback loop
problem: On a hot day your body temperature starts to rise above 37 °C. Describe how negative feedback brings it back.
step: The change is detected: nerve cells sense that body temperature is above its normal set point.
step: The control center, in the brain, signals the skin and sweat glands.
step: The response is sweating and widening of blood vessels in the skin, so more heat leaves the body.
step: As temperature falls back toward 37 °C, the signals weaken. The response reversed the original change, which is why it is called negative feedback.

@mistakes
- Using "organ" and "organ system" as if they mean the same thing; a system contains several organs.
- Believing each organ belongs to only one system; the pancreas, for example, helps digestion and also makes hormones.
- Thinking negative feedback means something bad; it simply means the response opposes the change.
