@article
Animals are living things. They need food, water, air and a safe place to live called **shelter**.

A **habitat** is the place where an animal lives and finds what it needs. Here are some habitats:

- **Ocean:** fish, whales and crabs live here.
- **Forest:** deer, owls and squirrels live here.
- **Desert:** it is hot and dry. Lizards and camels live here.
- **Pond:** frogs, ducks and turtles live here.
- **Farm:** cows, hens and pigs live here with help from people.

An animal's body fits its habitat. A fish has fins for swimming. A polar bear has thick fur for the cold. A duck has webbed feet for paddling.

### Animal homes

Some animals have special homes. A bird builds a nest. A bee lives in a hive. A rabbit digs a burrow.

@example
title: Where does a frog live?
problem: A frog has wet skin and strong back legs for jumping and swimming. Which habitat is best for it: a dry desert or a pond?
step: Frogs need water and wet places. A desert is dry.
step: A pond has water to swim in and plants to hide in. So the pond is the best habitat for a frog.

@mistakes
- Thinking every animal can live anywhere. Each animal needs a habitat that gives it the right food and shelter.
- Mixing up the words habitat and home. A habitat is the whole place. A nest or burrow is one kind of home in it.
