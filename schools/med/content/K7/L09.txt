@lesson
title: Sound and light
summary: Explain that sound comes from vibrations and that light helps us see.
objective: Say that sounds are made when things vibrate, or shake back and forth.
objective: Tell loud from quiet and high from low sounds.
objective: Say that we need light to see, and that some things block light and make shadows.
reading: NASA Space Place: look up "What is light?" or ask your grown-up to read a picture book about sound.

@article
A **sound** is made when something **vibrates**. To vibrate means to shake back and forth very fast.

Put your fingers on your throat and hum. Feel the buzz? That is your voice vibrating. Pluck a rubber band stretched around a box. You can see it shake, and you can hear a sound.

- A big, hard shake makes a **loud** sound.
- A small, gentle shake makes a **quiet** sound.
- A short, tight rubber band makes a **high** sound. A long, loose one makes a **low** sound.

### Light

We need **light** to see. The Sun, a lamp and a flashlight give off light. Light travels in straight lines.

Some things let light pass through, like clear glass. Some things **block** light, like a book. When an object blocks light, it makes a **shadow**.

### Try it

With your grown-up, use a flashlight in a dim room. Hold your hand in the beam and look at the shadow on the wall.

@example
title: A rubber band guitar
problem: Your grown-up stretches a rubber band around an empty box. You pluck it. Why do you hear a sound?
step: When you pluck the band, it shakes back and forth. That shaking is a vibration.
step: The vibration makes the air shake, and your ears hear a sound. When you touch the band to stop it, the sound stops.

@mistakes
- Thinking sound comes from nowhere. Sound always comes from something that vibrates.
- Thinking a shadow is a thing. A shadow is the dark place where light is blocked.
