@project
title: My little book
overview: You will make a five-page book. It has a beginning, a middle and an end. You write and draw it with your grown-up, then read it aloud to someone.
goal: Write a story with a beginning, a middle and an end.
goal: Write sentences with a capital letter, spaces and an end mark.
goal: Read your book aloud smoothly.
step: Fold three sheets of paper in half and staple or tie them to make a book, or use five separate pages.
step: Pick a character and a setting. For example, Pip the duck at the pond, or Max the dog in the yard.
step: Tell your grown-up your story with first, next, then and last.
step: Write one or two short sentences on each page. Say each word slowly and write its sounds. Copy sight words like "the" and "said" from your cards.
step: Draw a picture on each page that shows what the words say.
step: Page 1 is the beginning, pages 2 to 4 are the middle, and page 5 is the end.
step: Check each sentence: capital letter, spaces, end mark.
step: Read your book aloud to someone. Slide your finger under the words.
deliverable: A five-page book with words and pictures.
deliverable: A read-aloud of your book to a grown-up or a friend.
deliverable: Two sentences that name your characters and your setting.
rubric: The story has a clear beginning, middle and end.
rubric: The characters and setting can be named.
rubric: Each sentence has a capital letter, spaces and an end mark.
rubric: Each page has a picture that matches the words.
rubric: You read the book aloud and slid your finger under the words.
safety: Use only safe things like paper, crayons and a stapler. A grown-up helps with the stapler.
tutor: Can you help me think of a character and a setting for my story?
tutor: How do I stretch the sounds in a word so I can spell it?
tutor: Can you listen to me read my book and tell me one thing I did well?

@review
### Week by week

- **Week 1, letter names.** 26 letters. 5 vowels: a, e, i, o, u. Big and small letters.
- **Week 2, letter sounds.** Letters say sounds. Say them short and clean. c says /k/ in cat.
- **Week 3, rhyming and syllables.** Rhymes end the same (cat, hat). Clap one beat per syllable.
- **Week 4, blending.** Say each sound, then blend them into a word.
- **Week 5, short vowels.** cat, bed, pig, dog, sun.
- **Week 6, letter teams.** sh, ch, th and wh. Two letters, one sound.
- **Week 7, sight words.** Read the, was, said, of and others by sight.
- **Week 8, silent e.** cap becomes cape. The e makes the vowel say its name.
- **Week 9, sentences.** Capital letter at the start, spaces, an end mark.
- **Week 10, story parts.** Characters, setting and events.
- **Week 11, retelling.** Use first, next, then and last. Answer who, what, where and why.
- **Week 12, writing.** Write words by their sounds, then write sentences.

### Self-check

- I can say all 26 letters and a sound for each.
- I can clap syllables and blend three sounds.
- I can read short-vowel words and silent e words.
- I can read my sight words.
- I can read a story and retell it in order.
- I can write a sentence.

### Common errors

- Mixing up b and d.
- Saying "buh" instead of /b/.
- Splitting a letter team like sh.
- Forgetting that the silent e is silent.
- Forgetting the capital letter or the end mark.
