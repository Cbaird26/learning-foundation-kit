@article
**Slope** measures how steeply a line rises or falls. It is a **rate of change**: how much the output changes for each one-unit change in the input.

### Rise over run

Pick two points $(x_1, y_1)$ and $(x_2, y_2)$ on a line. The **rise** is the vertical change $y_2 - y_1$ and the **run** is the horizontal change $x_2 - x_1$. The slope is
$$m = \frac{\text{rise}}{\text{run}} = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}.$$
You may name the points in either order, but use the same order on top and bottom. For $(1, 2)$ and $(4, 8)$ the slope is $\frac{8 - 2}{4 - 1} = \frac{6}{3} = 2$.

### What the sign tells you

- **Positive slope:** the line rises from left to right.
- **Negative slope:** the line falls from left to right.
- **Zero slope:** a horizontal line; $y$ never changes.
- **Undefined slope:** a vertical line; the run is $0$ and you cannot divide by zero.

### Slope as a rate in context

When the axes have units, the slope has units too: (units of $y$) per (unit of $x$). Suppose a line on a graph of savings versus weeks passes through $(2, 50)$ and $(6, 130)$. The slope is $\frac{130 - 50}{6 - 2} = 20$ dollars per week. That is an **interpretation**: the savings grow by \$20 each week.

A good interpretation always names the quantity, the direction, the amount and the units. "The slope is $20$" is not enough; "the account gains \$20 for each additional week" is.

### Equal rates give a line

If the rate of change is the same between every pair of points, the points lie on a straight line. If the ratio $\frac{\Delta y}{\Delta x}$ changes from one pair of points to the next, the graph is not a line.

### Connection to equations

Slopes also show up when comparing two options. If one plan costs \$20 plus \$4 per visit and another costs \$8 plus \$6 per visit, the numbers \$4 and \$6 are the slopes of their cost lines. Setting the costs equal gives an equation with the variable on both sides, and its solution tells you where the two plans cost the same.

@example
title: Finding and reading a slope
problem: A line passes through $(2, 1)$ and $(6, 10)$. Find its slope. Then, if $x$ is hours and $y$ is centimeters of snow on the ground, say what the slope means.
step: Rise: $10 - 1 = 9$. Run: $6 - 2 = 4$.
step: Slope: $m = \frac{9}{4} = 2.25$. It is positive, so the line rises.
step: Interpretation: the snow depth increases by $2.25$ cm for each additional hour.

@mistakes
- Subtracting in different orders on top and bottom, which flips the sign.
- Computing run over rise instead of rise over run.
- Leaving out units, or saying "the slope is 20" without describing what changes.
- Confusing zero slope (horizontal) with undefined slope (vertical).

@practice num
prompt: Find the slope of the line through $(1, 2)$ and $(4, 8)$.
answer: 2
check: (8 - 2) / (4 - 1)
hint: Slope is rise over run: $\frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}$.
hint: The rise is $6$. What is the run?
solution: $\frac{8 - 2}{4 - 1} = \frac{6}{3} = 2$.

@practice num
prompt: Find the slope of the line through $(-2, 5)$ and $(4, -1)$.
answer: -1
check: (-1 - 5) / (4 - (-2))
hint: Be careful subtracting negative numbers.
hint: The rise is $-1 - 5 = -6$ and the run is $4 - (-2)$.
solution: $\frac{-1 - 5}{4 - (-2)} = \frac{-6}{6} = -1$.

@practice num
prompt: A sunflower is $12$ cm tall on day $2$ and $26$ cm tall on day $9$. If it grew at a constant rate, how many centimeters per day did it grow?
answer: 2
check: (26 - 12) / (9 - 2)
tol: 0.05
unit: cm/day
hint: The rate of change is the change in height divided by the change in days.
hint: The height changed by $14$ cm over $7$ days.
solution: $\frac{26 - 12}{9 - 2} = \frac{14}{7} = 2$ cm per day.

@practice num
prompt: Gym A charges a \$20 sign-up fee plus \$4 per visit. Gym B charges a \$8 sign-up fee plus \$6 per visit. For how many visits do the two gyms cost exactly the same?
answer: 6
check: (20 - 8) / (6 - 4)
unit: visits
hint: Write each total cost with $v$ visits, then set the two costs equal.
hint: $20 + 4v = 8 + 6v$. Collect the $v$ terms on one side.
solution: $20 + 4v = 8 + 6v$ gives $12 = 2v$, so $v = 6$. Both cost \$44 for $6$ visits. The slopes \$4 and \$6 are the cost per visit.

@quiz num
prompt: Find the slope of the line through $(3, -4)$ and $(7, 4)$.
answer: 2
check: (4 - (-4)) / (7 - 3)
hint: Use rise over run and keep the same point order top and bottom.
hint: The rise is $4 - (-4)$.
solution: $\frac{4 - (-4)}{7 - 3} = \frac{8}{4} = 2$.

prompt: Solve for $x$: $7x - 9 = 3x + 11$.
answer: 5

hint: Collect the $x$ terms on one side by subtracting $3x$.
hint: You get $4x - 9 = 11$.
