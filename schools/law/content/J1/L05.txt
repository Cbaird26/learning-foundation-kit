@article
**Negative numbers** are numbers less than zero. They describe things like a temperature of $-5^\circ$C, a diver at $-12$ meters (below sea level), or a bank balance of $-\$30$ (money owed).

### The number line

On a horizontal number line, zero is in the middle, positives go right and negatives go left. Numbers get **greater** as you move right. That means $-2 > -9$ even though $9$ looks bigger: $-2$ is farther right.

To put numbers in order, picture them on the line. From least to greatest: $-9, -2, 0, 4$.

### Opposites

Two numbers are **opposites** if they are the same distance from zero on opposite sides. The opposite of $5$ is $-5$, and the opposite of $-12$ is $12$. Writing the opposite uses a minus sign: $-(-12) = 12$. The opposite of $0$ is $0$.

### Absolute value

The **absolute value** of a number is its distance from zero. Write it with bars: $|-7| = 7$ and $|7| = 7$. Distance is never negative, so the absolute value is never negative.

Absolute value ignores direction, so $|-6| > |5|$ is true because $6 > 5$, while $-6 < 5$ because $-6$ is to the left.

### Distance on a number line

To find the distance between two numbers on a line, think about how many steps lie between them. From $-4$ to $6$ the distance is $4 + 6 = 10$: four steps to zero, then six more. When the numbers are on opposite sides of zero, add their absolute values. When they are on the same side, subtract the smaller absolute value from the larger.

### Meaning in context

A balance of $-\$30$ means you owe \$30. Its absolute value, $30$, tells you the size of the debt. Always connect the sign to a real meaning: below or above, owed or owned, lost or gained.

@example
title: Comparing and measuring with negatives
problem: Compare $-4$ and $-9$, and find the distance between them on a number line.
step: Place both on a number line. $-9$ is farther left than $-4$, so $-9 < -4$.
step: Both numbers are on the same side of zero. Their absolute values are $|-4| = 4$ and $|-9| = 9$.
step: Subtract the smaller from the larger: $9 - 4 = 5$. The distance between $-4$ and $-9$ is $5$.

@mistakes
- Thinking a negative number with a bigger digit is greater: $-9$ is less than $-2$.
- Saying the absolute value of $-7$ is $-7$; absolute value is a distance, so it is $7$.
- Adding the absolute values to find a distance when both numbers are on the same side of zero.

@quiz num
prompt: One diver is at $-18$ meters and another is at $-7$ meters, measured from the water surface. How many meters apart are they?
answer: 11
check: abs(-18 - (-7))
unit: m
hint: Both numbers are on the same side of zero.
hint: Subtract the smaller absolute value from the larger.
solution: $|-18| = 18$ and $|-7| = 7$, so the distance is $18 - 7 = 11$ meters.
