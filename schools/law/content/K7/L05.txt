@article
**Weather** is what the air outside is like today. It can be sunny, cloudy, rainy, windy or snowy. It can be hot, warm, cool or cold.

You can look out the window to see the weather. You can feel the wind on your face. A **thermometer** tells how warm or cold the air is.

### Weather chart

A **chart** helps you remember. Each day, draw a picture for the weather. At the end of the week, count how many days were sunny, how many were rainy, and so on.

### Seasons

The weather changes in a pattern through the year. We call the parts of this pattern **seasons**. There are four.

- **Spring:** warmer, with rain. Plants start to grow.
- **Summer:** the hottest time, with long days.
- **Fall:** cooler. Many leaves change color and fall.
- **Winter:** the coldest time, with short days. Some places have snow.

The seasons come in the same order every year: spring, summer, fall, winter, and then spring again.

@example
title: Read a weather chart
problem: A chart for 5 days shows: sunny, sunny, rainy, cloudy, sunny. How many days were sunny?
step: Go through the days one at a time. Day 1 is sunny. Day 2 is sunny. Day 3 is rainy, so skip it.
step: Day 4 is cloudy, so skip it. Day 5 is sunny.
step: Count the sunny days: 1, 2, 3. So 3 days were sunny.

@mistakes
- Mixing up weather and seasons. Weather is today's air. A season is a long part of the year.
- Mixing up the order of the seasons. After winter comes spring.

@quiz num
prompt: In one week, 4 days were sunny and 2 days were cloudy. The other days were rainy. How many days were rainy? A week has 7 days.
answer: 1
check: 7 - 4 - 2
hint: Add the sunny and cloudy days first, then take that from 7.
solution: 4 + 2 = 6, and 7 - 6 = 1. One day was rainy.
