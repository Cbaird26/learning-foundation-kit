@article
Shapes are all around you. A clock, a window and a slice of pizza all have shapes.

### Four shapes

- A **circle** is round. It has no sides and no corners. A plate and a coin are circles.
- A **square** has 4 sides that are all the same length, and 4 corners. A cracker can be a square.
- A **rectangle** has 4 sides and 4 corners. Two sides are long and two sides are short. A door and a book are rectangles.
- A **triangle** has 3 sides and 3 corners. A slice of pizza is like a triangle.

### Count the sides

Run your finger along a side and count: 1, 2, 3. A triangle has 3 sides. Do the same for a square: 1, 2, 3, 4. A square has 4 sides.

### Where is it?

We use position words to say where things are.

- **above**: higher than something
- **below**: lower than something
- **next to**: right beside something
- **between**: in the middle of two things

Put a cup on the table. The cup is **above** the floor. The floor is **below** the cup. Put a spoon on each side of the cup. The cup is **between** the spoons.

### Shape hunt

Look around your home. Can you find a circle, a square, a rectangle and a triangle?

@example
title: Name the shape
problem: A shape has 3 sides and 3 corners. What shape is it?
step: Count the sides: 3. Count the corners: 3.
step: A circle has no sides. A square and a rectangle have 4 sides.
step: A shape with 3 sides is a triangle.

@example
title: Where is the ball?
problem: A ball is on a shelf. A toy is on the floor under the shelf. Is the toy above or below the ball?
step: The ball is high up on the shelf. The toy is down on the floor.
step: The toy is lower than the ball, so the toy is below the ball.

@mistakes
- Calling a rectangle a square. A square has 4 sides that are all the same length.
- Forgetting that a circle has no corners.
- Mixing up above and below.
