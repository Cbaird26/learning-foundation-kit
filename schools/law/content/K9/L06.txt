@article
A **government** is a group of people who make rules, keep order and provide services for a community. Taxes pay for many of these services. **Taxes** are money people pay to the government.

### Local government

Local government works for a city, town or county. Its leaders may include:

- A **mayor**, who leads a city or town and carries out its rules.
- A **city council** (or town council), a group that makes local rules called **ordinances**. The council members are chosen by voters.

Local government pays for things like parks, libraries, roads, trash pickup, and police and fire services.

### State government

There are 50 states. Each state has its own government. A state has a **governor**, who leads the state. A state also has a **legislature**, a group that makes state laws. State courts explain the law when people disagree about it.

State governments help with things like state roads, state parks, and schools.

### Three jobs of government

Governments do three kinds of jobs:

1. **Make** rules (a council or legislature).
2. **Carry out** rules (a mayor or governor).
3. **Explain** rules when there is a disagreement (courts and judges).

### Voting

Grown-up citizens vote to choose many leaders. Kids can still help by learning, asking questions and caring for their community.

@example
title: Who makes the rule?
problem: A town wants a rule that dogs must be on a leash in the park. Who makes this local rule?
step: This is a local rule for one town.
step: The town or city council makes local rules.
step: So the council makes the rule.

@example
title: City or state?
problem: Who leads a state: a mayor or a governor?
step: A mayor leads a city or town.
step: A governor leads a whole state.
step: The answer is a governor.

@mistakes
- Mixing up the mayor and the governor. A mayor leads a city; a governor leads a state.
- Thinking one person makes all rules. Groups like councils and legislatures make rules, and courts explain them.
- Thinking government services are free for no reason. Taxes help pay for them.
