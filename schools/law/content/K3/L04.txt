@article
Two fractions are **equivalent** if they name the same amount, even though they look different.

### See it with a picture

Draw a bar and cut it into $2$ equal pieces. Shade $1$ piece. That is $\frac{1}{2}$.

Now cut the same bar into $6$ equal pieces. To get the same shaded part you shade $3$ pieces. That is $\frac{3}{6}$.

The shaded part is the same size, so $\frac{1}{2} = \frac{3}{6}$.

### The rule

Multiply the top number and the bottom number by the **same** number. The fraction stays the same size.

$$\frac{1}{2} = \frac{1 \times 3}{2 \times 3} = \frac{3}{6}$$

You are cutting each piece into smaller equal pieces, so you have more pieces but each one is smaller.

### Simplest form

You can go the other way. Divide the top and bottom by the same number. This is called **simplifying**.

$$\frac{6}{9} = \frac{6 \div 3}{9 \div 3} = \frac{2}{3}$$

A fraction is in **simplest form** when the top and bottom have no shared factor except $1$. Factors are the key here. List the factors of both numbers and look for one they share. This is why we practiced factors last week.

### Factor pairs up to 100

Factor pairs help you find shared factors fast. For $48$ the pairs are $1 \times 48$, $2 \times 24$, $3 \times 16$, $4 \times 12$ and $6 \times 8$. For $100$ the pairs are $1 \times 100$, $2 \times 50$, $4 \times 25$, $5 \times 20$ and $10 \times 10$.

### Why it matters

To add fractions next week, we need them to have the same bottom number. Equivalent fractions let us change the bottom number without changing the amount.

@example
title: Make and simplify equivalent fractions
problem: Write $\frac{2}{5}$ with a bottom number of $15$. Then simplify $\frac{18}{24}$.
step: To get from $5$ to $15$ we multiply by $3$. Do the same on top: $2 \times 3 = 6$. So $\frac{2}{5} = \frac{6}{15}$.
step: For $\frac{18}{24}$, find a shared factor. Both $18$ and $24$ are in the $6$ times table, so divide both by $6$.
step: $18 \div 6 = 3$ and $24 \div 6 = 4$. So $\frac{18}{24} = \frac{3}{4}$. Nothing but $1$ divides both $3$ and $4$, so it is in simplest form.

@mistakes
- Adding the same number to the top and bottom. Only multiplying or dividing by the same number keeps the fraction equal.
- Changing the bottom number but forgetting to change the top.
- Stopping too early when simplifying, for example leaving $\frac{6}{12}$ as $\frac{3}{6}$ when it can become $\frac{1}{2}$.

@quiz num
prompt: Simplify $\frac{8}{12}$. Enter the answer as a fraction.
answer: 2/3
check: 8 / 12
hint: Find a number that divides both $8$ and $12$.
hint: Both are in the $4$ times table. Divide the top and bottom by $4$.
solution: $8 \div 4 = 2$ and $12 \div 4 = 3$, so $\frac{8}{12} = \frac{2}{3}$.

@quiz mc

solution: $\frac{2}{8}$, $\frac{3}{12}$ and $\frac{4}{16}$ come from multiplying $\frac{1}{4}$ by $2$, $3$ and $4$. For $\frac{5}{24}$ the bottom is multiplied by $6$ but the top by $5$, so it is not equal.
