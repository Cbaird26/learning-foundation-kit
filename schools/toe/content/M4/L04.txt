@article
A subspace can be described by a list of vectors that spans it, but some lists are wasteful. A **basis** is a spanning list with no waste.

### Independence

Vectors $\mathbf{v}_1, \dots, \mathbf{v}_k$ are **linearly independent** if the only solution of
$$c_1\mathbf{v}_1 + \cdots + c_k\mathbf{v}_k = \mathbf{0}$$
is $c_1 = \cdots = c_k = 0$. Otherwise one vector is a combination of the others, and that is the waste. Place the vectors in the columns of a matrix $A$. They are independent exactly when $N(A) = \{\mathbf{0}\}$, which means every column has a pivot.

### Basis and dimension

A **basis** of a space is a list of vectors that is independent and spans the space. Every vector in the space is then a combination of the basis vectors in **exactly one way**. All bases of a space have the same number of vectors, and this number is the **dimension**. The standard basis shows $\dim \mathbb{R}^n = n$, and $\{1, x, x^2\}$ shows that the polynomials of degree at most $2$ form a space of dimension $3$.

In an $n$-dimensional space, any $n$ independent vectors form a basis, and any list of more than $n$ vectors is dependent.

### Bases from row reduction

Reduce $A$ to echelon form (or to the fully reduced form $R$).

- **Column space.** A basis is the set of **pivot columns of the original $A$**. The pivot columns of $R$ would be wrong: row operations keep the dependencies between columns but change the columns themselves.
- **Null space.** Each free variable gives one **special solution**. Set that free variable to $1$, the other free variables to $0$, and solve for the pivot variables. The special solutions form a basis of $N(A)$.

### Rank-nullity

Suppose $A$ has $n$ columns and $r$ pivots. Then $\dim C(A) = r$ and $\dim N(A) = n - r$, so
$$\dim C(A) + \dim N(A) = n.$$
Every column is either a pivot column, which adds to the column space, or a free column, which adds a special solution.

```python
import numpy as np
A = np.array([[1, 2, 0, 1], [2, 4, 1, 3], [3, 6, 1, 4]])
r = np.linalg.matrix_rank(A)
print(r, A.shape[1] - r)   # 2 2
```

@example
title: Bases for both subspaces
problem: Find a basis for $C(A)$ and for $N(A)$, where $A = \begin{bmatrix}1&2&0&1\\2&4&1&3\\3&6&1&4\end{bmatrix}$.
step: Eliminate: $R_2 \to R_2 - 2R_1$ gives $[0\ 0\ 1\ 1]$ and $R_3 \to R_3 - 3R_1$ gives $[0\ 0\ 1\ 1]$. Then $R_3 \to R_3 - R_2$ gives a zero row.
step: The echelon rows are $[1\ 2\ 0\ 1]$ and $[0\ 0\ 1\ 1]$. The pivots are in columns $1$ and $3$, so $r = 2$, and $x_2$ and $x_4$ are free.
step: Column space: the pivot columns of the **original** $A$ are $(1, 2, 3)$ and $(0, 1, 1)$. They form a basis of $C(A)$.
step: Null space: the rows say $x_1 = -2x_2 - x_4$ and $x_3 = -x_4$. Take $(x_2, x_4) = (1, 0)$ to get $(-2, 1, 0, 0)$, and $(x_2, x_4) = (0, 1)$ to get $(-1, 0, -1, 1)$.
step: Check: $A(-1, 0, -1, 1)^T = (-1 + 0 + 0 + 1,\ -2 + 0 - 1 + 3,\ -3 + 0 - 1 + 4) = (0, 0, 0)$. Rank-nullity: $2 + 2 = 4$ columns.

@example
title: Testing independence
problem: Are $\mathbf{v}_1 = (1, 2, 1)$, $\mathbf{v}_2 = (2, 1, 0)$, $\mathbf{v}_3 = (4, 5, 2)$ independent?
step: Put the vectors in the columns of $A = \begin{bmatrix}1&2&4\\2&1&5\\1&0&2\end{bmatrix}$ and eliminate.
step: $R_2 \to R_2 - 2R_1$ gives $[0\ -3\ -3]$ and $R_3 \to R_3 - R_1$ gives $[0\ -2\ -2]$. Then $R_3 \to R_3 - \tfrac23 R_2$ gives a zero row.
step: There are only two pivots, so column $3$ is free and the vectors are **dependent**.
step: To find the dependency, set $x_3 = 1$. Then $-3x_2 - 3 = 0$ gives $x_2 = -1$, and $x_1 + 2(-1) + 4 = 0$ gives $x_1 = -2$. So $-2\mathbf{v}_1 - \mathbf{v}_2 + \mathbf{v}_3 = \mathbf{0}$, that is $\mathbf{v}_3 = 2\mathbf{v}_1 + \mathbf{v}_2$. Check: $2(1, 2, 1) + (2, 1, 0) = (4, 5, 2)$.

@mistakes
- Taking the pivot columns of the reduced matrix $R$, rather than of the original $A$, as the basis of the column space.
- Setting all free variables to $1$ at once. Special solutions use one free variable equal to $1$ and the others equal to $0$.
- Claiming that three nonzero vectors in $\mathbb{R}^3$ are automatically independent. Independence needs three pivots.
