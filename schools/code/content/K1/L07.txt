@article
You can count past 20. The numbers keep going all the way to 100.

### Count to 100

After 20 comes 21, 22, 23 and so on. After 29 comes 30. After 39 comes 40. There is a pattern: each new group of numbers starts with a new "ty" word.

20, 30, 40, 50, 60, 70, 80, 90, 100

### Count by tens

Counting by tens is a fast way to count. Say 10, 20, 30, 40, 50. Each number is 10 more than the one before. Ten is a good group to count because each bundle has 10 in it.

If you have 4 bundles of 10 sticks, you have 40 sticks. Say it by tens: 10, 20, 30, 40.

### Before and after

The number **after** is one more. The number **before** is one less. Right after 45 comes 46. Right before 45 comes 44.

### A hundred chart

A hundred chart has 10 rows, and each row has 10 numbers. The last number in each row is a number you say when you count by tens. Look at it with your grown-up and find 10, 20, 30.

### Count things

Count out 30 beans by making 3 piles of 10. Then count the piles by tens: 10, 20, 30.

@example
title: Counting by tens
problem: You count by tens: 10, 20, 30, 40. What number comes next?
step: Each number is 10 more than the one before.
step: After 40 you say 50.

@example
title: One more
problem: What number comes right after 59?
step: Say the numbers in order: 57, 58, 59.
step: After 59 comes 60. This is a new ty word.

@mistakes
- Saying 20, 30, 40, 50 but then skipping a ty word, like going from 59 to 70.
- Counting by ones when you should count by tens.
- Mixing up before (one less) and after (one more).
