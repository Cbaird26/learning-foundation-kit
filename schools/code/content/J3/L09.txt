@article
Geometry gives you reliable facts about angles and shapes. Once you know a few of them, you can find unknown measurements without a ruler.

### Triangle angle facts

The three interior angles of any triangle add to $180^\circ$. If two angles are $47^\circ$ and $68^\circ$, the third is $180 - 47 - 68 = 65^\circ$.

An **exterior angle** is formed by extending one side. The **exterior angle theorem** says it equals the sum of the two **remote interior angles** (the two interior angles not next to it). If those two are $50^\circ$ and $60^\circ$, the exterior angle is $110^\circ$.

### Parallel lines and a transversal

A **transversal** is a line that crosses two parallel lines. It makes eight angles, in matching positions at each crossing.

- **Corresponding angles** (same position at each crossing) are equal.
- **Alternate interior angles** (between the lines, on opposite sides) are equal.
- **Alternate exterior angles** are equal.
- **Same-side interior angles** add to $180^\circ$.
- **Vertical angles** (opposite at one crossing) are always equal, and angles that form a straight line add to $180^\circ$.

### Congruent and similar

Figures are **congruent** if one can be moved onto the other by rigid motions, so all matching sides and angles are equal. Figures are **similar** if one can be mapped onto the other by rigid motions and a dilation: same shape, possibly different size. Matching angles are equal, and matching sides are in the same ratio, the **scale factor**.

### The AA similarity criterion

Two triangles are similar if **two pairs of angles are equal**, since the third pair must then be equal as well (the angles of each triangle sum to $180^\circ$). This is called **AA similarity**.

### Using similarity

If triangles are similar, set up a proportion of corresponding sides. Suppose a triangle with sides $6$ and $8$ is similar to one in which the side matching $6$ is $9$. The scale factor is $\frac{9}{6} = 1.5$, so the side matching $8$ is $8 \times 1.5 = 12$.

Always match sides carefully: the side opposite a given angle in one triangle corresponds to the side opposite the equal angle in the other. Sketching and labeling both triangles prevents most errors.

@example
title: Showing triangles are similar
problem: Triangle $ABC$ has angles $A = 40^\circ$ and $B = 75^\circ$. Triangle $DEF$ has angles $D = 75^\circ$ and $E = 65^\circ$. Are the triangles similar?
step: Find the missing angle in each triangle using the angle sum: $C = 180 - 40 - 75 = 65^\circ$ and $F = 180 - 75 - 65 = 40^\circ$.
step: Match equal angles: $B = D = 75^\circ$, $C = E = 65^\circ$ and $A = F = 40^\circ$.
step: Two (indeed all three) pairs of angles are equal, so by AA the triangles are similar, with correspondence $A \leftrightarrow F$, $B \leftrightarrow D$, $C \leftrightarrow E$.

@mistakes
- Matching sides in the order the letters are written instead of by the equal angles.
- Assuming equal angles mean congruent; similar triangles can have different sizes.
- Treating same-side interior angles as equal when they add to $180^\circ$.
- Forgetting the angle sum when only two angles of a triangle are given.

prompt: The two remote interior angles of a triangle are $50^\circ$ and $60^\circ$. Find the exterior angle at the third vertex, in degrees.
answer: 110
check: 50 + 60
unit: degrees
hint: The exterior angle theorem says the exterior angle equals the sum of the remote interior angles.
hint: Add $50$ and $60$.
solution: $50 + 60 = 110$. Alternatively, the third interior angle is $70^\circ$ and the exterior angle is $180 - 70 = 110^\circ$.

@practice num
prompt: Two parallel lines are cut by a transversal. A pair of alternate interior angles measure $(3x + 10)^\circ$ and $70^\circ$. Find $x$.
answer: 20
check: (70 - 10) / 3
hint: Alternate interior angles are equal.
hint: Solve $3x + 10 = 70$.
solution: $3x + 10 = 70$ gives $3x = 60$ and $x = 20$.

@practice num
prompt: Triangle $ABC$ is similar to triangle $DEF$. Side $AB = 6$ and $BC = 8$, and the matching side $DE = 9$. Find $EF$.
answer: 12
check: 8 * 9 / 6
hint: Find the scale factor from the pair of matching sides you know.
hint: $\frac{DE}{AB} = \frac{9}{6} = 1.5$.
solution: $EF = BC \times 1.5 = 8 \times 1.5 = 12$.

choice*: They are similar, because the third angles are $70^\circ$ and $30^\circ$ and all three angle measures match.

@quiz num
prompt: The angles of a triangle are $x^\circ$, $2x^\circ$ and $3x^\circ$. Find $x$.
answer: 30
check: 180 / (1 + 2 + 3)
hint: The angles must add to $180^\circ$.
hint: $x + 2x + 3x = 6x$.
solution: $6x = 180$, so $x = 30$ (angles $30^\circ, 60^\circ, 90^\circ$).

@quiz num
prompt: A vertical stick $1.5$ m tall casts a shadow $2$ m long. At the same time a tree casts a shadow $12$ m long. How tall is the tree, in meters?
answer: 9
check: 1.5 * 12 / 2
unit: m
tol: 1%
hint: The sun makes the same angle with the ground for both, so the triangles are similar.
hint: Height over shadow is the same for both: $\frac{1.5}{2} = \frac{h}{12}$.
solution: $h = 12 \times \frac{1.5}{2} = 9$ m.
