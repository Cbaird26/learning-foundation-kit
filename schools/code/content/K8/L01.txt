@lesson
title: Life cycles
summary: Name the stages in the life cycles of plants, insects, frogs and mammals and put them in order.
objective: Say what a life cycle is and put its stages in order.
objective: Tell complete metamorphosis (4 stages) from incomplete metamorphosis (3 stages).
objective: Compare the life cycle of a plant, an insect and a mammal.
reading: NASA Space Place: look up the topic "life on Earth" and "what is a life cycle" for ideas about living things; for the main lesson, watch a plant or an insect near your home.

@article
Every living thing is born or starts, grows, makes young of its own, and one day dies. This pattern is called a **life cycle**. Then the young grow up and the cycle starts again.

### A plant
Many plants begin as a **seed**. With water, warmth and a little soil, the seed sprouts into a small **seedling**. The seedling grows into an adult plant. The adult makes flowers. Flowers become fruit with new seeds inside. Those seeds can start new plants.

### An insect
A butterfly changes its body shape a lot. This is called **complete metamorphosis**. It has 4 stages:

1. **Egg**
2. **Larva** (a caterpillar that eats and eats)
3. **Pupa** (the chrysalis, where the body changes)
4. **Adult** (a butterfly with wings)

A grasshopper has only 3 stages: egg, **nymph**, adult. A nymph looks like a tiny grasshopper without full wings. This is **incomplete metamorphosis**.

### A frog
A frog starts as an egg in water. It hatches into a **tadpole** with a tail and gills. Then it grows legs and is a **froglet**. The froglet loses its tail and becomes an adult frog that breathes air. That makes 4 stages: egg, tadpole, froglet and adult.

### A mammal
A **mammal** has hair or fur and feeds its babies milk. A dog is born alive, not from an egg. A puppy drinks milk, grows, and becomes an adult dog that can have puppies.

### What is the same?
All of them are born or hatch, grow, become adults and make young. The stages are different, but the order always stays the same.

@example
title: Put a butterfly's life in order
problem: A caterpillar is in a chrysalis. What stage is it in, and what comes next?
step: A butterfly's stages are egg, larva, pupa, adult.
step: The chrysalis is the pupa stage.
step: After pupa comes adult, so a butterfly comes out next.

@example
title: Count the stages
problem: A frog has 4 stages. A grasshopper has 3. How many stages do they have in all?
step: Write the numbers: $4$ and $3$.
step: Add them: $4 + 3 = 7$.

@mistakes
- Thinking that the caterpillar and the butterfly are two different animals. They are the same animal at two stages.
- Mixing up the order, such as putting the pupa before the larva.
- Thinking all animals hatch from eggs. Mammals such as dogs are born alive.

@practice num

answer: 15
check: 5 * 3
hint: There are 5 groups of 3. What operation helps?
solution: $5 \times 3 = 15$ seeds.
