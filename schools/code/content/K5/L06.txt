@article
**Sequence** means the order that things happen. If you know the order, you can follow a story or a set of directions.

### Clue words for order

Look for these words: **first, next, then, after that, finally, last**. They tell you what comes when. Time words like "in the morning" and "at night" help too.

Here is a sequence for making toast. First, put bread in the toaster. Next, push the lever down. Then wait for it to pop up. Finally, add butter.

### Cause and effect

A **cause** is why something happens. An **effect** is what happens.

- Cause: It rained all day.
- Effect: The grass got wet.

Ask two questions. "What happened?" That is the effect. "Why did it happen?" That is the cause.

### Clue words for cause and effect

Look for **because, so, since** and **as a result**. "The cup fell **because** the cat bumped it." The cause is the cat bumping it. The effect is the cup falling.

### Making a story map

A story map is a drawing or list of the events in order, in boxes with arrows. Write one short event in each box: first, next, then, last. Then pick one event and say what caused it.

@example
title: One cause and its effect
problem: Read: "Mo forgot his coat. It was cold outside. Mo shivered, so he ran back to get it." What is one cause and its effect?
step: Ask what happened. Mo shivered and ran back. That is an effect.
step: Ask why. It was cold and he forgot his coat. That is the cause.
step: Say it with because: "Mo ran back because he was cold."

@mistakes
- Mixing up cause and effect. The cause comes first in time, because it makes the effect happen.
- Leaving out a middle event when listing the order.
- Using only one clue word and missing the others.
