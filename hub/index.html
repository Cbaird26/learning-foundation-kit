How every school works

The schools share one method, so once you know one, you know them all.

Lessons that teach

Every course has twelve weekly lessons. Each lesson has a short reading, worked examples, the common mistakes to avoid and pointers to free readings.

Practice that checks your answers

Numbers, multiple choice, typed answers and, in some schools, Python code are checked on the spot, with hints and full solutions when you need them.

Mastery you can see

Get a lesson's practice right first time without hints to reach Proficient. Quizzes and tests can take a skill to Mastered.

Start where you are

A starting-point check suggests where to begin. Each course also has a challenge with one question from every week: score about 85% and you can move past the course.

Mistakes come back until they stick

Every missed question goes into an error log and returns after a delay, until you get it right and it counts as repaired.

A tutor for every lesson

The Tutor button prepares a prompt about what you are studying that you can copy into a chat with Claude.

Good to know

Where is my progress saved?
In this browser on this device, separately for each school. Keep using the same browser and do not clear its site data. Another browser or device starts fresh, and nothing is ever uploaded.
Do the schools share courses?
Many elementary, middle and high school courses appear in several schools. Each school keeps its own record, so a course you finished in one school starts fresh in another; its course challenge lets you move past it quickly.
Do I get credit or a degree?
No. These are self-study schools, and scores do not award credit, degrees or any credential. Exam, licensing and admissions rules change, so check current details with the organizations that run them.
What does it cost?
Nothing. There is no account, no sign-up and nothing to install.
